## 1. Agentic Harness Engineering Beta: Requirement-Centered Evolution of Agent Harnesses / Agentic Harness Engineering Beta：以需求为中心的 Agent Harness 演进

**Date**: 2026-11-18 | **arXiv**: [2611.24924v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24924v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.24924v1)

**Categories**: cs.SE, cs.AI

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Real-world harness engineering is driven by explicit and implicit user requirements, which guide development and validation. However, existing harness evolution methods often overlook implicit requirements and interaction needs, while using a single evolution agent to modify the harness and benchmark scores to select changes. This process often produces narrow patches and leads to benchmark-specific local optima. We introduce Agentic Harness Engineering Beta (AHE-Beta), a requirement-centered loop that brings real-world engineering practices into harness evolution. The loop consists of three steps: (1) User-driven evaluation lets simulated users raise their requirements as issues while they work with the harness over multiple turns. (2) Team-based modification implements those requirements as generalizable changes rather than task-specific patches. (3) Requirement-driven selection merges a change only when it resolves a verified requirement and passes review. Empirically, on held-out tasks, AHE-Beta raises pass@1 from 21.9% to 33.3% while using 40.8% fewer tokens than Base Harness, and achieves higher pass@1 than Claude Code, Meta-Harness, and AHE. In a blind human A/B test, real users prefer AHE-Beta in 31 of 48 valid pairs and rate it higher on all five dimensions, matching the directional preference of the user simulator. The human A/B results show that AHE-Beta achieves its goal of aligning harness evolution with real-world user requirements. Without further evolution, the gains transfer to GLM-5.3 and DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash with pass@1 improvements of 15.8% and 5.3% over Base Harness. Together, these results show that requirement-centered harness evolution is a more general method for improving agent capabilities.

现实世界的线束工程是由显式和隐式用户需求驱动的，这些需求指导着开发和验证。然而，现有的线束演化方法常常忽略隐含的需求和交互需求，同时使用单个演化代理来修改线束和基准分数以选择更改。此过程通常会产生狭窄的补丁并导致特定于基准的局部最优。我们推出 Agentic Harness Engineering Beta (AHE-Beta)，这是一个以需求为中心的循环，可将现实世界的工程实践引入线束演进中。该循环由三个步骤组成：(1) 用户驱动的评估让模拟用户在多次使用线束时提出他们的要求作为问题。 (2) 基于团队的修改将这些要求实现为可通用的更改，而不是特定于任务的补丁。 (3) 需求驱动选择仅当解决了已验证的需求并通过审核时才合并变更。根据经验，在保留任务上，AHE-Beta 将 pass@1 从 21.9% 提高到 33.3%，同时使用的令牌比 Base Harness 少 40.8%，并且比 Claude Code、Meta-Harness 和 AHE 实现了更高的 pass@1。在盲人 A/B 测试中，真实用户在 48 个有效对中的 31 个中更喜欢 AHE-Beta，并在所有五个维度上对其评分较高，这与用户模拟器的方向偏好相匹配。人类 A/B 结果表明，AHE-Beta 实现了使线束进化与现实世界用户需求保持一致的目标。无需进一步演进，增益就会转移到 GLM-5.3 和 DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash，pass@1 比 Base Harness 分别提高 15.8% 和 5.3%。总之，这些结果表明，以需求为中心的线束演化是提高代理能力的更通用方法。

## 2. Ground, Iterate, Trace: Decomposing AI Research Ideation / 基础、迭代、追踪：分解人工智能研究理念

**Date**: 2026-11-18 | **arXiv**: [2611.24887v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24887v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.24887v1)

**Categories**: cs.CL

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Large language models have been widely used to generate plausible research ideas, yet it remains unclear what makes automated ideation effective and how generated novel proposals arise from prior scientific work. We introduce the Creative AI Researcher (CAR), an agentic framework combining literature grounding, iterative development and explicit conceptual operators. CAR constructs a semantic knowledge graph from retrieved literature and equips an ideation agent with graph-retrieval tools and four operators: bisociation, analogy, exploration and elaboration. Each operator call is checked against structural acceptance conditions and recorded, producing a trace of intermediate transformations and declared literature sources. We evaluate CAR with two language-model backbones across 40 LiveIdeaBench research topics and two execution benchmarks. CAR achieves significantly higher methodological novelty (MN) than single-shot prompting and The AI Scientist. Its methodological novelty does not differ significantly from the Research Ideation Agent, while its feasibility scores are higher on both backbones. With a shared experiment executor, CAR achieves the highest pairwise win rate among the evaluated AI methods on FML-bench-Lite and BioXArena. In a ten-condition ablation study, MN point estimates favor CAR over one-step proposal on both backbones, but no MN contrast survives correction for multiple comparisons. The results do not establish a consistent quality benefit from explicit operators beyond free-form iteration. CAR makes the sequence of operations and declared literature sources inspectable, separating judged idea quality from procedural traceability.

大型语言模型已被广泛用于生成看似合理的研究想法，但目前尚不清楚是什么使自动构思有效，以及如何从先前的科学工作中产生新颖的建议。我们引入了创意人工智能研究员（CAR），这是一个结合文献基础、迭代开发和明确概念运算符的代理框架。 CAR 从检索到的文献中构建语义知识图，并为构思代理配备图检索工具和四个运算符：二元关联、类比、探索和阐述。每个操作员调用都会根据结构验收条件进行检查并记录，从而产生中间转换和声明的文献来源的痕迹。我们使用跨 40 个 LiveIdeaBench 研究主题的两个语言模型主干和两个执行基准来评估 CAR。与单次提示和人工智能科学家相比，CAR 实现了显着更高的方法新颖性 (MN)。其方法新颖性与研究构思代理没有显着差异，而其在两个主干上的可行性得分都较高。通过共享实验执行器，CAR 在 FML-bench-Lite 和 BioXArena 上评估的 AI 方法中实现了最高的配对获胜率。在一项十条件消融研究中，在两个主干上，MN 点估计优于 CAR，而不是一步建议，但没有 MN 对比能够通过多重比较的校正。结果并没有从自由形式迭代之外的显式运算符中建立一致的质量优势。 CAR 使操作顺序和声明的文献来源可检查，将判断的想法质量与程序可追溯性分开。

</details>

## 3. Local AI Agents in Power Electronics Simulation: Deployment, Measurements and Costs / 电力电子仿真中的本地人工智能代理：部署、测量和成本

**Date**: 2026-11-18 | **arXiv**: [2611.24880v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24880v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.24880v1)

**Categories**: cs.AI, eess.SY

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Agentic artificial intelligence (AI) is emerging in power electronics, where it drives the simulation loop that engineers used to close largely by hand. In this loop, a converter design, or a study of the network it connects to, is built, run, measured and revised until it satisfies its specification. Such an agent can be served from a cloud API or from an inference device on the premises. For organizations whose designs, customer data or export-controlled material cannot leave the site, and whose simulators are licensed there, local deployment is the natural choice. In such a deployment, the agent becomes an electrical load on the organization's own equipment, i.e., every run consumes power and energy on a local device, generates tokens and takes time. However, existing work focuses mainly on what the agent can achieve with cloud-hosted models. What a local deployment costs in power, energy, tokens and time on real simulation tasks, and what its choices trade against one another, has received little quantitative investigation under comparable conditions. To address this gap, a systematic evaluation framework is presented in this paper. It applies locally served models to agentic power electronics tasks, from the converter level to the system level, and measures their power, energy, token and time costs. Based on these measurements, the capability boundary of a local agent on such tasks and the cost per solved task are characterized. The results are expected to provide practical insight for the use of agentic AI in power electronics and the power systems it serves.

代理人工智能 (AI) 正在电力电子领域兴起，它驱动工程师过去主要手动关闭的模拟循环。在这个循环中，转换器设计或对其连接的网络的研究被构建、运行、测量和修改，直到满足其规格。此类代理可以通过云 API 或本地推理设备提供服务。对于设计、客户数据或出口管制材料无法离开现场且模拟器已获得许可的组织来说，本地部署是自然的选择。在这样的部署中，代理成为组织自身设备上的电力负载，即每次运行都会消耗本地设备上的电力和能源，生成令牌并花费时间。然而，现有的工作主要集中在代理可以通过云托管模型实现的目标。在实际模拟任务中，本地部署在电力、能源、代币和时间方面的成本，以及其选择之间的相互影响，在可比条件下几乎没有得到定量调查。为了解决这一差距，本文提出了一个系统的评估框架。它将本地服务模型应用于从转换器级到系统级的代理电力电子任务，并测量其功率、能源、令牌和时间成本。基于这些测量，本地代理在此类任务上的能力边界和每个解决任务的成本被表征。研究结果预计将为电力电子及其服务的电力系统中代理人工智能的使用提供实用的见解。

## 4. Learning to Interact in Multi-Agent Self-Play for Controllable Autonomous Driving Scenario Generation / 学习多智能体自我对弈中的交互以生成可控的自动驾驶场景

**Date**: 2026-11-18 | **arXiv**: [2611.24876v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24876v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.24876v1)

**Categories**: cs.RO, cs.LG

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Large-scale, multi-agent self-play trains robust driving policies effectively in simulation, but controlling these models to generate specific, safety-critical edge cases remains a key challenge. Existing goal-conditioned self-play frameworks rely primarily on static map-location goals and fail to reliably produce complex agent interactions such as aggressive tailgating or adversarial cut-offs. These agent interactions are necessary for rigorous autonomous vehicle (AV) testing. To address this, we present an extended multi-agent self-play framework that introduces agent-relative dynamic goals. By conditioning policies on this extended goal representation, our framework enables targeted, controllable scenario synthesis. We demonstrate that our approach successfully generates interactive driving behaviors that static-goal baselines cannot simulate, providing a flexible tool for long-tail scenario generation in AV testing.

大规模、多智能体自我对弈可以在模拟中有效地训练稳健的驾驶策略，但控制这些模型以生成特定的、安全关键的边缘情况仍然是一个关键挑战。现有的目标条件自我游戏框架主要依赖于静态地图位置目标，并且无法可靠地产生复杂的代理交互，例如积极的尾随或对抗性切断。这些代理交互对于严格的自动驾驶汽车 (AV) 测试是必要的。为了解决这个问题，我们提出了一个扩展的多智能体自我博弈框架，该框架引入了与智能体相关的动态目标。通过根据这种扩展的目标表示调整策略，我们的框架可以实现有针对性的、可控的场景合成。我们证明了我们的方法成功地生成了静态目标基线无法模拟的交互式驾驶行为，为 AV 测试中的长尾场景生成提供了灵活的工具。

## 5. Vibe-Trading: Towards Agent-Native Trading / Vibe-Trading：迈向代理原生交易

**Date**: 2026-11-18 | **arXiv**: [2611.24845v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24845v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.24845v1)

**Categories**: cs.AI, cs.CL, cs.MA, q-fin.CP, q-fin.TR

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Existing AI agents can retrieve information, call tools, run experiments, and produce multi-step outputs. Yet most are built around relatively static workflows and struggle to preserve reliable state as the environment changes. Financial trading makes this limitation especially consequential: prices, news, market conditions, portfolio positions, and available capital evolve continuously. A result is useful only when it remains tied to the correct time, data snapshot, and execution context. We present Vibe-Trading, a comprehensive agent-native trading framework and all-in-one workspace for building, evaluating, and evolving agents for financial trading. It unifies market research, financial data, strategy generation, backtesting, portfolio analysis, paper trading, multi-agent collaboration, and optional broker execution. Its central contribution is an architecture that keeps results, evidence, research history, and trading authority connected throughout the research-to-action process. Each result retains its source, temporal scope, configuration, and producing run. Shared evidence grounding enables verification, versioned research records support reproducible continuation, and an explicit authority gate separates strategy research from live execution. Together, these designs provide a practical foundation for trading agents whose results can be traced, reproduced, and acted on only under explicit authority.

现有的人工智能代理可以检索信息、调用工具、运行实验并产生多步骤输出。然而，大多数都是围绕相对静态的工作流程构建的，并且随着环境的变化而努力保持可靠的状态。金融交易使这一限制变得尤为重要：价格、新闻、市场状况、投资组合头寸和可用资本不断变化。仅当结果与正确的时间、数据快照和执行上下文相关时，它才有用。我们推出 Vibe-Trading，这是一种全面的代理原生交易框架和一体化工作区，用于构建、评估和发展金融交易代理。它统一了市场研究、财务数据、策略生成、回溯测试、投资组合分析、模拟交易、多代理协作和可选经纪商执行。其核心贡献是一个架构，使结果、证据、研究历史和交易权威在整个研究到行动的过程中保持联系。每个结果都保留其来源、时间范围、配置和生产运行。共享证据基础可以进行验证，版本化的研究记录支持可重复的延续，并且明确的权威门将策略研究与实时执行分开。这些设计共同为交易代理提供了实用的基础，其结果只能在明确的授权下进行追踪、复制和操作。

## 6. Kaer: Security-Aware Replanning for Multi-Agent Systems / Kaer：多代理系统的安全感知重新规划

**Date**: 2026-11-18 | **arXiv**: [2611.24839v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24839v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.24839v1)

**Categories**: cs.MA, cs.LG

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

One popular architecture for multi-agent systems (MAS) relies on an orchestrator that generates an initial plan, coordinates its execution, and dynamically revises the plan based on agent feedback when tasks cannot be fully specified in advance. This dynamic replanning introduces a critical vulnerability: malicious agents can embed instructions within their feedback to hijack the replanning process. Existing defenses focus exclusively on per-tool access control or static, pre-execution policy enforcement, leaving the replanning phase unsecured.   We introduce Kaer, a multi-agent framework featuring security-aware replanning. Kaer shields the planner from untrusted agent feedback by conveying diagnostics to the replanning procedure strictly through abstract templates. Plan revisions are restricted to a set of deterministic repair operators, and all modified plans must satisfy preset policies. We create Cintra, a benchmark of simulated user tasks requiring replanning paired with adversarial objectives targeting the replanning phase. Using Cintra, we evaluate both task completion rates and system vulnerability to replanning exploits by malicious agents. Evaluating Kaer on Cintra against Magentic-One (an undefended MAS) and ControlValve (the state-of-the-art defense) demonstrates that Kaer achieves higher robustness than both systems while maintaining comparable utility to ControlValve. Although abstract failure templates slightly constrain utility on select tasks, Kaer enables MAS to perform secure, dynamic plan revisions without exposing the orchestrator to untrusted agent feedback.

多代理系统 (MAS) 的一种流行架构依赖于一个编排器，该编排器生成初始计划、协调其执行，并在无法提前完全指定任务时根据代理反馈动态修改计划。这种动态重新规划引入了一个严重漏洞：恶意代理可以在其反馈中嵌入指令来劫持重新规划过程。现有的防御措施仅专注于每个工具的访问控制或静态的执行前策略实施，从而使重新规划阶段变得不安全。   我们介绍 Kaer，一个具有安全感知重新规划功能的多代理框架。 Kaer 通过抽象模板严格将诊断信息传递给重新规划过程，从而保护规划者免受不受信任的代理反馈的影响。计划修订仅限于一组确定性修复操作员，并且所有修改的计划必须满足预设策略。我们创建了 Cintra，这是一个需要重新规划的模拟用户任务的基准，并与针对重新规划阶段的对抗性目标相结合。使用 Cintra，我们评估任务完成率和系统对恶意代理重新规划利用的脆弱性。在 Cintra 上对 Kaer 与 Magentic-One（一种不设防的 MAS）和 ControlValve（最先进的防御）进行评估表明，Kaer 比这两个系统实现了更高的鲁棒性，同时保持了与 ControlValve 相当的实用性。尽管抽象故障模板稍微限制了选定任务的实用性，但 Kaer 使 MAS 能够执行安全、动态的计划修订，而不会让编排器暴露于不受信任的代理反馈。

## 8. Depth Before Breadth: Reverse-to-Forward Curriculum with Advantage Decomposition for LLM Agent / 先深度后广度：LLM代理优势分解的逆向前进课程

**Date**: 2026-11-18 | **arXiv**: [2611.24697v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24697v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.24697v1)

**Categories**: cs.AI

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Reinforcement learning (RL) has become a common approach for training large language model (LLM) agents on long-horizon tasks. However, learning from sparse terminal rewards makes credit assignment and exploration difficult. We approach these challenges through a policy-induced capability region, the set of states from which the current policy can reliably complete a task. Near its boundary, an agent may need only a few actions to reach a more solvable state, even when the goal remains distant. This reduces long-horizon learning to two coupled subproblems of reaching that state and completing the task from there. We address them with paired advantages and a progressive reverse-to-forward curriculum. The advantages provide separate signals for reaching more solvable states and completing the task from them, while the curriculum expands the capability region backward along successful reference trajectories and then into off-reference states through on-policy exploration. On ALFWorld and WebShop, our method outperforms competitive agentic RL methods at both model scales, achieving success rates of 90.1%/96.1% and 80.2%/82.6% with Qwen2.5-1.5B/7B-Instruct, respectively.

强化学习 (RL) 已成为在长期任务上训练大型语言模型 (LLM) 代理的常用方法。然而，从稀疏的终端奖励中学习使得信用分配和探索变得困难。我们通过政策诱导的能力区域来应对这些挑战，即当前政策可以可靠地完成任务的一组状态。在其边界附近，即使目标仍然遥远，智能体可能只需要几个动作就可以达到更容易解决的状态。这将长期学习减少为两个耦合的子问题：达到该状态并从那里完成任务。我们通过成对的优势和渐进的从前到后的课程来解决这些问题。这些优点为达到更多可解状态并完成任务提供了单独的信号，而课程则沿着成功的参考轨迹向后扩展能力区域，然后通过在策略探索进入非参考状态。在 ALFWorld 和 WebShop 上，我们的方法在两个模型规模上都优于竞争性代理 RL 方法，在 Qwen2.5-1.5B/7B-Instruct 上分别实现了 90.1%/96.1% 和 80.2%/82.6% 的成功率。

## 9. Piccolo: Co-Evolving Decision Models and Data through Recursive Self-Improvement / Piccolo：通过递归自我完善共同进化决策模型和数据

**Categories**: cs.CL, cs.AI, cs.CV

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>
