## 1. Delegent: A Multi-Agent Harness That Reduces Cost without Sacrificing Performance / Delegent：一种多代理线束，可在不牺牲性能的情况下降低成本

**Date**: 2026-11-24 | **arXiv**: [2611.33098v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33098v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.33098v1)

**Categories**: cs.AI, cs.IR

**Code**: https://github.com/castorini/delegent

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Agents built on frontier language models are expensive. At every step, the frontier model re-reads its entire context and executes every subtask regardless of its difficulty. This paper explores the simple thesis that much of this cost can be removed by delegating easy subtasks to a small language model, or SLM, that runs on hardware one already owns. To test it, we build Delegent, a simple and effective agent harness built on Claude Code, in which the frontier model stays in charge of every task but hands focused subtasks, such as investigating code, running tests, and searching a corpus, to SLM subagents. Existing approaches such as cascades and model routers usually choose which model executes the entire task, whereas in Delegent, the frontier model always leads the execution and delegates easy subtasks to the SLM. No router or model is trained, so either model can be swapped freely. With the help of a 27B FP8 SLM, Delegent reduces the frontier cost of long-horizon coding by 67% and of agentic search by 59%, with no loss in task performance, and even a 2B SLM that fits on a laptop reduces the coding cost by 66%. The savings grow with the legwork that the frontier model would otherwise do itself, and a short instruction, which leaves the main coding tasks to the frontier model, accounts for most of the saving and keeps the 2B SLM from losing tasks. Code: https://github.com/castorini/delegent

基于前沿语言模型构建的代理非常昂贵。在每一步中，前沿模型都会重新读取其整个上下文并执行每个子任务，无论其难度如何。本文探讨了一个简单的论点，即通过将简单的子任务委托给在已有硬件上运行的小型语言模型（SLM），可以消除大部分成本。为了测试它，我们构建了 Delegent，这是一个基于 Claude Code 构建的简单而有效的代理工具，其中前沿模型负责每项任务，但将重点子任务（例如调查代码、运行测试和搜索语料库）交给 SLM 子代理。现有的方法（例如级联和模型路由器）通常选择哪个模型执行整个任务，而在 Delegent 中，前沿模型始终主导执行并将简单的子任务委托给 SLM。没有训练路由器或模型，因此任何一个模型都可以自由交换。在 27B FP8 SLM 的帮助下，Delegent 将长视野编码的前沿成本降低了 67%，将代理搜索的前沿成本降低了 59%，并且任务性能没有损失，甚至适用于笔记本电脑的 2B SLM 也将编码成本降低了 66%。节省的成本随着前沿模型本身需要做的跑腿工作而增加，而一条简短的指令将主要的编码任务留给前沿模型，节省了大部分成本，并防止 2B SLM 丢失任务。代码：https://github.com/castorini/delegent

</details>

## 2. Hybrid AI for AI-Native RAN Control: A Functional Perspective / 用于 AI-Native RAN 控制的混合 AI：功能视角

**Date**: 2026-11-24 | **arXiv**: [2611.33035v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33035v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.33035v1)

**Categories**: cs.AI

**Code**: https://github.com/pegahani/Hybrid-AI-RAN-Survey.

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

AI-native radio access network (RAN) control involves interdependent decisions across network entities, protocol layers, and timescales. Understanding how AI and conventional methods can jointly support these decisions requires a common basis for comparing their functional roles. This survey introduces a technique-agnostic taxonomy of RAN decision-making organized around four complementary capabilities: Representation and Reasoning, Prediction and Simulation, Adaptive Control, and Decision Synthesis. These capabilities are defined by their roles in the control process, independently of the techniques used to implement them. Through this perspective, we examine representative AI and model-based approaches, including foundation models, world models, reinforcement learning, and optimization. We then apply the taxonomy to representative RAN control solutions across slow, medium, fast, and cross-timescale applications to identify which capabilities they implement and how these capabilities are combined in hybrid AI systems. We also review datasets, benchmarks, simulators, digital twins, and experimental platforms according to their support for the decision-making process and reproducible evaluation. The review highlights a gap between the availability of resources for closed-loop experimentation and the standardization of tasks, metrics, and evaluation protocols needed for meaningful comparisons.Finally, we identify research directions for hybrid AI in AI-native RAN, including capability integration, multi-agent and cross-timescale coordination, constraint handling, and reproducible closed-loop evaluation. A curated list of surveyed works and evaluation resources is available at https://github.com/pegahani/Hybrid-AI-RAN-Survey.

AI 原生无线接入网络 (RAN) 控制涉及跨网络实体、协议层和时间尺度的相互依赖的决策。了解人工智能和传统方法如何共同支持这些决策需要一个共同的基础来比较它们的功能角色。这项调查引入了一种与技术无关的 RAN 决策分类法，该分类法围绕四种互补功能进行组织：表示和推理、预测和模拟、自适应控制和决策综合。这些功能是根据它们在控制过程中的角色来定义的，与用于实现它们的技术无关。通过这个视角，我们研究了代表性的人工智能和基于模型的方法，包括基础模型、世界模型、强化学习和优化。然后，我们将分类法应用于慢速、中速、快速和跨时间尺度应用程序的代表性 RAN 控制解决方案，以确定它们实现的功能以及这些功能如何在混合 AI 系统中组合。我们还根据数据集、基准、模拟器、数字孪生和实验平台对决策过程和可重复评估的支持来审查它们。该评论强调了闭环实验资源的可用性与有意义比较所需的任务、指标和评估协议的标准化之间的差距。最后，我们确定了 AI-native RAN 中混合 AI 的研究方向，包括能力集成、多智能体和跨时间尺度协调、约束处理和可重复的闭环评估。调查作品和评估资源的精选列表可在 https://github.com/pegahani/Hybrid-AI-RAN-Survey 获取。

## 3. Embodied AI Fails the Mirror Test: How a Simple Mirror Breaks Robot Policies / 嵌入式人工智能未能通过镜子测试：简单的镜子如何破坏机器人策略

**Date**: 2026-11-24 | **arXiv**: [2611.33010v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33010v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.33010v1)

**Categories**: cs.CV, cs.AI, cs.RO

**Code**: https://github.com/iPPLab/robodojo-mirror.

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Can a simple mirror expose the limitations of today's robotic manipulation policies? Inspired by the mirror self-recognition test in animal cognition, we investigate whether robot policies can reliably manipulate physical objects in scenes containing their reflections. We introduce RoboDojo-Mirror, a benchmark built on RoboDojo that preserves task objectives, success criteria, and policy checkpoints while comparing a mirror-free baseline with single-mirror, dual-mirror, and mirrored-tabletop conditions. Across five policies spanning Vision-Language-Action models, World Action Models, and agentic policies, every mirror condition reduces aggregate success rates: averaged over the three conditions, the drop ranges from 10.8 to 56.7 percentage points across policies, and it reaches 62.5 points on the mirrored tabletop. Because a mirror is also a physical obstacle, we compare it against geometrically matched non-reflective panels; 50--88\% of mirror-specific failures occur without recorded robot--panel contact, so direct collisions alone do not explain the degradation. Trajectory and contact analyses instead reveal recurring behaviors consistent with phantom targeting of reflected objects and spatial mislocalization of real ones. Targeted diagnostics further characterize grasping near reflections, policy-dependent pushing progress, and physical-target interaction under main-camera occlusion. Real-robot experiments with $π_{0.5}$ and FastWAM also show lower overall success rates under the upright-mirror and mirrored-tabletop conditions. These findings reveal a shared vulnerability to reflective environments across diverse robot policies and motivate reflection-aware evaluation of embodied intelligence. Code is available at https://github.com/iPPLab/robodojo-mirror.

一面简单的镜子能否暴露当今机器人操纵政策的局限性？受到动物认知中镜子自我识别测试的启发，我们研究了机器人策略是否能够可靠地操纵包含反射的场景中的物理对象。我们介绍 RoboDojo-Mirror，这是一个基于 RoboDojo 构建的基准测试，它保留任务目标、成功标准和策略检查点，同时将无镜像基准与单镜像、双镜像和镜像桌面条件进行比较。在涵盖愿景-语言-行动模型、世界行动模型和代理策略的五项政策中，每种镜像条件都会降低总成功率：三种条件的平均成功率下降幅度从 10.8 到 56.7 个百分点，在镜像桌面上达到 62.5 个百分点。由于镜子也是一种物理障碍，因此我们将其与几何匹配的非反射面板进行比较； 50--88% 的镜子特定故障是在没有记录的机器人与面板接触的情况下发生的，因此仅直接碰撞并不能解释退化。相反，轨迹和接触分析揭示了与反射物体的幻影瞄准和真实物体的空间错误定位一致的重复行为。有针对性的诊断进一步表征了捕捉近反射、依赖策略的推动进展以及主摄像头遮挡下的物理目标交互。使用 $π_{0.5}$ 和 FastWAM 进行的真实机器人实验也显示，在直立镜面和镜面桌面条件下，总体成功率较低。这些发现揭示了不同机器人策略对反射环境的共同脆弱性，并激发了对体现智能的反射感知评估。代码可在 https://github.com/iPPLab/robodojo-mirror 获取。

## 4. Attestation is All You Need: Toward a Zero-Trust Architecture for HPC AI Agents / 您所需要的就是证明：迈向 HPC AI 代理的零信任架构

**Date**: 2026-11-24 | **arXiv**: [2611.32958v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32958v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.32958v1)

**Categories**: cs.CR, cs.AI

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

AI agents are beginning to automate data analysis, orchestration, and simulation steering in HPC environments, but they introduce a security problem that existing cluster defenses do not address: the hijacked authorized agent. An adversary can subvert an agent through prompt injection, poisoned shared data, compromised tools, or multi-agent coordination, then exfiltrate sensitive data through legitimate encrypted LLM API traffic. We propose behavioral attestation as a runtime defense for this setting. Our system, AEGIS, compiles task-derived behavioral constraints, monitors agent actions with ebpf-based syscall interception, verifies evidence against constraint profiles, correlates behavior across agents, and triggers slurm-level containment. On the real attestation path, AEGIS detects all four implemented attack classes with sub-second median latency at a 1-second interval and low measured verifier overhead. Controlled experiments show full attack-suite coverage, stronger detection than representative baselines, and no observed false positives on the evaluated benign workflows.

AI 代理开始在 HPC 环境中实现数据分析、编排和模拟引导的自动化，但它们引入了现有集群防御无法解决的安全问题：被劫持的授权代理。攻击者可以通过即时注入、中毒的共享数据、受损的工具或多代理协调来破坏代理，然后通过合法的加密 LLM API 流量窃取敏感数据。我们建议使用行为证明作为此设置的运行时防御。我们的系统 AEGIS 编译任务派生的行为约束，使用基于 ebpf 的系统调用拦截来监视代理操作，验证约束配置文件的证据，关联代理之间的行为，并触发 slurm 级别的遏制。在真实的证明路径上，AEGIS 以 1 秒的间隔检测所有四种已实施的攻击类别，中值延迟为亚秒级，并且测得的验证器开销较低。对照实验显示了完整的攻击套件覆盖，比代表性基线更强的检测，并且在评估的良性工作流程中没有观察到误报。

</details>

## 5. SOARS: System One Assisted Reranking for Agentic Search / SOARS：系统一辅助代理搜索重新排名

**Date**: 2026-11-24 | **arXiv**: [2611.32917v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32917v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.32917v1)

**Categories**: cs.IR, cs.CL

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Deep search agents address complex information needs through iterative retrieval and reasoning, where the relevance of retrieved evidence influences downstream performance. Existing rerankers improve retrieval effectiveness but introduce substantial latency and cost when invoked throughout the search trajectory. We introduce SOARS, a training-free framework leveraging a System One model for fast, calibrated relevance estimation. At each search step, SOARS selects query-relevant passages from long documents and reranks candidates in a single call, jointly considering the original question and agent-generated sub-query. Across six LLM backbones on BrowseComp-Plus and WebShaper, SOARS consistently improves answer accuracy and evidence recall while reducing search calls, end-to-end latency, and inference cost. On BrowseComp-Plus, it improves GPT-5.4 Mini's accuracy from 73.9% to 84.7% while reducing search calls from 23 to 14, demonstrating the potential of System One reranking for more effective and efficient deep search.

深度搜索代理通过迭代检索和推理来满足复杂的信息需求，其中检索到的证据的相关性影响下游性能。现有的重新排序器提高了检索效率，但在整个搜索轨迹中调用时会带来大量的延迟和成本。我们引入 SOARS，这是一种免培训框架，利用 System One 模型进行快速、校准的相关性估计。在每个搜索步骤中，SOARS 从长文档中选择与查询相关的段落，并在一次调用中对候选者重新排序，同时考虑原始问题和代理生成的子查询。通过 BrowseComp-Plus 和 WebShaper 上的六个 LLM 主干，SOARS 持续提高答案准确性和证据回忆，同时减少搜索调用、端到端延迟和推理成本。在 BrowseComp-Plus 上，它将 GPT-5.4 Mini 的准确率从 73.9% 提高到 84.7%，同时将搜索调用从 23 次减少到 14 次，展示了 System One 重新排名在更有效和高效的深度搜索方面的潜力。

</details>

## 6. Atomic Policy: Composing Atomic Manipulation Skills with Agentic Reasoning / 原子策略：将原子操作技能与代理推理结合起来

**Categories**: cs.RO

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Long-horizon manipulation requires robots to translate task intent into physical interactions as the scene changes. Vision-language models (VLMs) offer semantic and spatial reasoning, while visuomotor policies provide fine-grained control. Yet ambiguous goals and dependencies between actions make their integration challenging. We introduce Atomic Policy, a framework that composes learned manipulation skills through grounded steering and execution feedback. A VLM estimates the symbolic state and goal, and a PDDL solver constructs a skill plan. An agentic harness steers each skill through language, visual pointing, or both, and verifies its outcome to guide phase transitions and replanning. A common phase-level interface supports specialized policies and generalist action models. Experiments in simulation and on a real robot show how Atomic Policy improves task following and supports recovery in complex, long-horizon manipulation. ## 7. Breaking the Joint Action Space Dependence in Distributionally Robust Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning / 打破分布式鲁棒多智能体强化学习中的联合动作空间依赖

**Date**: 2026-11-24 | **arXiv**: [2611.32812v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32812v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.32812v1)

**Categories**: cs.LG

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

We study the sample complexity of learning robust coarse correlated equilibria (CCE) in finite-horizon, general-sum, distributionally robust Markov games with known rewards and a generative model of the nominal transitions. Each player evaluates a policy by its worst-case return over total-variation (TV) balls centered at the nominal transition distribution of every stage, state, and joint action; this is the standard $(s,a)$-rectangular uncertainty set. Existing generative-model guarantees for this model scale with the number $\prod_iA_i$ of joint actions, where $A_i$ is the number of actions of player~$i$. Since this number grows exponentially with the number of players, these guarantees suffer from the curse of multiagency. We design an algorithm whose sample complexity depends on the action sets only through the largest individual action-set size $A_*:=\max_iA_i$. The algorithm uses $\widetilde O(SA_*H^5D^4/\varepsilon^4)$ transition samples and, with probability at least $1-δ$, returns a Markov policy that is an $\varepsilon$-robust CCE from every initial state. Here $S$ is the number of states, $H$ is the horizon, and $D:=\max_i\min\{H,1/ρ_i\}$, where $ρ_i$ is the TV radius of player~$i$. Two ingredients make this possible: a plug-in estimate of the robust backup whose mean error does not depend on the number of states, and a single batch of samples per round that serves as bandit feedback for all players at once. The result shows that the dependence on $\prod_iA_i$ can be avoided without modifying the $(s,a)$-rectangular uncertainty set.

我们研究了在有限视野、一般和、具有已知奖励和名义转换生成模型的分布鲁棒马尔可夫游戏中学习鲁棒粗相关均衡（CCE）的样本复杂性。每个参与者通过以每个阶段、状态和联合行动的名义转移分布为中心的总变分 (TV) 球的最坏情况回报来评估策略；这是标准的$(s,a)$-矩形不确定性集。现有的生成模型保证该模型的规模为联合动作的数量 $\prod_iA_i$，其中 $A_i$ 是玩家 ~$i$ 的动作数量。由于这个数字随着玩家数量呈指数增长，因此这些保证受到了多机构的诅咒。我们设计了一种算法，其样本复杂度仅通过最大的单个动作集大小 $A_*:=\max_iA_i$ 取决于动作集。该算法使用 $\widetilde O(SA_*H^5D^4/\varepsilon^4)$ 转换样本，并以至少 $1-δ$ 的概率返回马尔可夫策略，该策略是每个初始状态的 $\varepsilon$ 鲁棒 CCE。这里$S$是状态数，$H$是地平线，$D:=\max_i\min\{H,1/ρ_i\}$，其中$ρ_i$是玩家~$i$的电视半径。有两个因素使这成为可能：对稳健备份的插件估计，其平均误差不依赖于状态数量，以及每轮的一批样本，同时作为所有玩家的强盗反馈。结果表明，在不修改$(s,a)$-矩形不确定性集的情况下，可以避免对$\prod_iA_i$的依赖。

</details>

## 8. RubikSQL-HB: Agentic Text-to-SQL with Knowledge in the Loop / RubikSQL-HB：具有循环知识的代理文本到 SQL

**Date**: 2026-11-24 | **arXiv**: [2611.32772v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32772v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.32772v1)

**Categories**: cs.AI

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Database agents need more than schema structure: business meanings, stored values and reference queries supply knowledge that becomes relevant at different points in reasoning. We introduce RubikSQL-HB, which exposes these materials through queryable, database-specific knowledge workspaces built on the HeavenBase platform. Its knowledge interface remains available as an agent constructs and investigates an answer. Candidate agents interleave knowledge access with SQL construction and execution; a downstream Judge can reuse the same interfaces to investigate disagreements. A two-stage example interface separates discovering a reference question from expanding its SQL. On a 500-question BIRD Mini-Dev evaluation, the full pipeline reaches 75.2% execution accuracy, against 71.2% for first-candidate selection and 70.4% for execution-majority voting, with a correct candidate available on 79.0% of questions. The raw-knowledge condition, which also regenerates candidates without the built workspace, is 3.0 points lower, and adjudication with tools and revision accounts for most of the gain over selection baselines. A recorded execution probe shows how continued investigation resolves a candidate disagreement. Separate archived runs show semantic revision recovering answers absent from their original candidate pools. A versioned development study further illustrates how recurring corrections become reusable execution checks. The system and results connect knowledge-interface design to agent decision quality: queryable knowledge supports the construction of useful alternatives, and evidence-aware adjudication connects those alternatives to a final answer. Keeping knowledge in the loop lets agents acquire relevant observations as their reasoning develops.

数据库代理需要的不仅仅是模式结构：业务含义、存储值和参考查询提供在推理的不同点变得相关的知识。我们引入了 RubikSQL-HB，它通过在 HeavenBase 平台上构建的可查询的、特定于数据库的知识工作区来公开这些材料。当代理构建和调查答案时，其知识界面仍然可用。候选代理将知识访问与 SQL 构建和执行交织在一起；下游法官可以重用相同的接口来调查分歧。两阶段示例界面将发现参考问题与扩展其 SQL 分开。在 500 个问题的 BIRD Mini-Dev 评估中，整个管道的执行准确率达到 75.2%，而第一候选选择的执行准确度为 71.2%，执行多数投票的执行准确度为 70.4%，79.0% 的问题都有正确的候选。原始知识条件（也可以在没有构建工作空间的情况下重新生成候选人）降低了 3.0 分，而使用工具和修订的裁决占了超过选择基线的大部分收益。记录的执行调查显示了持续调查如何解决候选人的分歧。单独存档的运行显示语义修订恢复了原始候选池中缺少的答案。版本化开发研究进一步说明了重复性更正如何成为可重用的执行检查。该系统和结果将知识界面设计与代理决策质量联系起来：可查询的知识支持构建有用的替代方案，而证据感知的裁决将这些替代方案与最终答案联系起来。保持知识的循环可以让代理在推理发展时获得相关的观察结果。

Cross-harness task supervision or rehearsal alone yields little gain, but their combination yields gains far larger than either: on BFCL V3 multi-turn with Qwen3-4B, they achieve 8.5% and 32.5% respectively, versus 58.8% together. Finally, on-policy reinforcement learning (RL) is more robust than distillation to single-harness training: it does not substantially degrade performance on other harnesses, and interface competence acquired through multi-harness distillation persists through subsequent RL.

LLM 代理通过定义系统提示、操作格式、反馈循环和资源限制的工具进行操作。模型可能拥有解决任务所需的知识，但无法通过特定的工具表达该知识。这就提出了一个基本问题：当策略在给定的工具下失败时，它是否缺少任务知识或操作界面的能力？我们区分代理能力的两个组成部分：任务能力（解决任务所需的知识和推理）和界面能力（通过给定的工具对知识采取行动的能力）。通过四个推理和代理基准，我们表明，根据它们如何相互作用，利用条件训练会产生截然不同的结果。多线束蒸馏将通过不同线束收集的轨迹中的任务知识聚合到共享策略中，但只有当训练保留或提高推理时间接口上的能力时，才能实现这些知识。否则，单线束训练可能会干扰接口能力，有时尽管基础任务知识有所改善，但仍会导致悬挂线束上的性能发生灾难性的崩溃。为了解开任务和界面监督，我们引入了工具排练：在练习目标界面时对携带最少任务知识的痕迹进行蒸馏。交叉线束任务监督或排练单独产生的收益很小，但它们的组合产生的收益远远大于其中任何一种：在 BFCL V3 与 Qwen3-4B 的多回合上，它们分别实现了 8.5% 和 32.5%，而合计实现了 58.8%。最后，在策略强化学习 (RL) 比单线束训练的蒸馏更加稳健：它不会大幅降低其他线束的性能，并且通过多线束蒸馏获得的接口能力会在后续的 RL 中持续存在。

</details>

## 33. What People Choose Not to Say: A Multimodal Benchmark for Fine-Grained Information Manipulation in Conversations / 人们选择不说的话：对话中细粒度信息操纵的多模式基准

**Date**: 2026-11-23 | **arXiv**: [2611.31346v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31346v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.31346v1)

**Categories**: cs.AI, cs.CL

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Recent advances in MLLMs have improved social understanding, enabling models to recognize emotions and distinguish truthful from deceptive behaviors. However, existing benchmarks largely focus on coarse-grained emotion recognition or binary truth-deception detection, overlooking the diverse ways people manipulate information in conversations, such as withholding, leakage, fabrication, evasion, and defensive counterclaim. Understanding these behaviors requires reasoning about underlying facts, participant knowledge, and conversational context, making them difficult to evaluate reliably. To address this gap, we introduce InfoCon, a multimodal benchmark for fine-grained information manipulation in conversations. InfoCon identifies six fine-grained strategies and derives a coarse consistency label from their factual and interactional relations. We develop an evidence-grounded multi-agent annotation framework to construct and verify reliable labels from rich contextual evidence. We benchmark representative multimodal models and systematically analyze their fine-grained information manipulation understanding, providing a testbed beyond binary truth-deception judgments.

MLLM 的最新进展提高了社会理解，使模型能够识别情绪并区分真实行为和欺骗行为。然而，现有的基准主要侧重于粗粒度的情感识别或二元真相欺骗检测，忽视了人们在对话中操纵信息的多种方式，例如隐瞒、泄漏、捏造、逃避和防御性反诉。理解这些行为需要对潜在事实、参与者知识和对话上下文进行推理，这使得它们难以可靠地评估。为了解决这一差距，我们引入了 InfoCon，这是一种用于在对话中进行细粒度信息操作的多模式基准。 InfoCon 确定了六种细粒度策略，并从它们的事实和交互关系中得出粗略的一致性标签。我们开发了一个基于证据的多代理注释框架，以从丰富的上下文证据中构建和验证可靠的标签。我们对代表性的多模态模型进行基准测试，并系统地分析它们对细粒度信息操纵的理解，提供超越二元真假判断的测试平台。

## 34. Retrieval-Augmented Generation in the Agentic Era: Tradition, Present, and Prospect / 代理时代的检索增强一代：传统、现在与前景

**Categories**: cs.CL

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) couples frozen language models with external, updatable knowledge, so that generation is grounded in retrieved evidence rather than in parametric memory alone. 检索增强生成（RAG）将冻结的语言模型与外部可更新的知识结合起来，因此生成基于检索到的证据，而不仅仅是参数记忆。这项工作通过将 RAG 连接到单个生命周期轴上来映射代理时代的 RAG，从离线索引到检索前、检索、检索后以及这些阶段的编排。它将索引和编排视为一流的组件，将代理工作流程视为自己的范例，并且涵盖了自这些评论以来出现的代理工作。除了这些组成部分之外，它还涵盖评估、应用以及有待未来探索的问题。本书从代理时代的角度系统地回顾了RAG，从传统到现在以及超越。

## 35. SHAKTI: Evidence-Grounded Conversational AI for Nutrition and Health / SHAKTI：基于证据的对话式人工智能对营养和健康的影响

**Date**: 2026-11-23 | **arXiv**: [2611.29988v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.29988v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.29988v1)

**Categories**: cs.CL, cs.IR

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Large language models (LLMs) answer many diet and health questions but can state unsupported claims and cite sources that do not support them. We present SHAKTI (Smart Health Advisor for Knowledge-based Tailored Interventions), a conversational assistant that answers only from retrieved scholarly papers and exact food-composition data, and evaluate it with retrieval, generation, grounding, efficiency and contextawareness metrics. We indexed 484,368,086 OpenAlex works, searched exactly on a GPU for evaluation (10,065 NFCorpus, BioASQ, SciFact and PubMedQA questions) and through a compressed 230-million-abstract index in deployment. On 1,000 stratified questions, we compared single-shot, multi-hop, corrective and deep-research retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) with Claude Sonnet. SHAKTI adds conversational query rewriting, USDA FoodData Central lookups, source-link checks and online verification of quotes and numbers. It was tested in 36 scripted conversational turns, a 248-question memory-depth test and a 38-question load test. Claims were checked by a Claude Opus judge against exactly the text the system saw. MedCPT reranking of hybrid retrieval gave the best MRR (0.21) and MAP@100 (0.14). Across existing RAG methods, faithfulness was 0.95-0.96, claim hallucination 0.5-0.8% and context precision up to 0.98; the deep-research agent answered 73.2% of BioASQ questions correctly. In conversation, SHAKTI reached a context-awareness score of 97.1%, faithfulness 95.5%, 1.9% unsupported claims, no contradicted claims and 229/229 working source links; it resolved references to the first question after 16 unrelated questions. An answer cost 14,716 tokens (US$0.06) with a median latency of 24 s. Large-scale retrieval with agentic evidence selection and mechanical verification yields auditable, low-hallucination nutrition answers.
