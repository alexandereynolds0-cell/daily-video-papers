## 1. WorldFlow: Joint Video and 3D Motion Prediction via Large-Scale Pretraining / WorldFlow ：通过大规模预训练实现联合视频和3D运动预测

**Date**: 2026-10-29 | **arXiv**: [2610.39278v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.39278v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.39278v1)

**Categories**: cs.CV, cs.AI, cs.RO

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Predicting future physical states stands as a central challenge in embodied intelligence. Existing video world models leave 3D dynamics implicit, action-based formulations remain tied to specific robot configurations, and 3D motion prediction models use flow-only supervision, providing a limited learning signal for physical dynamics. To circumvent these limitations, we introduce \method{}, a generative world model that jointly predicts future video and query-based 3D trajectories. By parameterizing 3D motion as continuous displacement tokens aligned with the video grid, a unified Mixture-of-Transformers (MoT) backbone jointly denoises both modalities under a rectified-flow objective. This formulation couples explicit geometric dynamics with visual evolution, effectively unlocking the physical commonsense embedded within pretrained video models. To empirically scale and validate this unified architecture, we train \method{} on a heterogeneous mixture of 1.44 million object-level samples spanning simulated and real-world domains. We further introduce WorldFlowBench, comprising 1,678 evaluation samples from 11 data sources across simulated and real-world interactions, including spatial reasoning tasks. Across all three evaluation sets, WorldFlow achieves the lowest average displacement error (ADE) and final displacement error (FDE) among the evaluated learning-based methods. Notably, on the challenging SpatialCog set, WorldFlow achieves an ADE of 0.060 m, a 54.2\% reduction compared with the strongest external baseline. It also reduces ADE and FDE by 55.9\% and 68.5\%, respectively, compared with its flow-only variant. Project page: https://worldflow-rbs.github.io/.

## 2. RAIBO-Wagon: Real-Time Navigation System for a Legged Robot Towing a Wagon / RAIBO-Wagon ：腿式机器人拖车实时导航系统

**Date**: 2026-10-29 | **arXiv**: [2610.39267v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.39267v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.39267v1)

**Categories**: cs.RO

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Legged robots have demonstrated advanced capabilities across a wide range of real-world tasks, yet much of this progress concerns the robot controlling only itself. Once the robot is coupled to an external object -- particularly an underactuated one -- the dynamics of the combined assembly become more complex. Towing a wagon is a common practical scenario, where coupled dynamics make reliable navigation challenging. This difficulty is amplified in confined spaces, where collision-free navigation requires finding a narrow set of feasible paths under strict real-time constraints. To address this, we propose a navigation framework that combines Conditional Flow Matching (CFM) with Model Predictive Path Integral (MPPI) control over a learned Forward Dynamics Model (FDM). The CFM model mitigates the initialization problem of MPPI and accelerates its convergence, while MPPI with the FDM recovers feasible solutions beyond the CFM model's training distribution by iterative refinement. We integrate the framework into a full-stack navigation system for RAIBO-Wagon, a quadruped towing a custom wagon, and validate it in real-world outdoor experiments on 15 m-long courses through confined spaces, with all sensing and computation performed fully onboard. The system achieves an 85% success rate across 40 trials, towing a total load of up to 40 kg -- nearly 100% of RAIBO's body weight.

腿式机器人在各种真实世界的任务中展示了先进的能力，但这些进展很大程度上与机器人只控制自己有关。 一旦机器人耦合到外部物体（特别是欠驱动物体） ，组合组件的动态就会变得更加复杂。 拖车是一种常见的实用场景，其中耦合动力学使得可靠的导航具有挑战性。 这种困难在密闭空间中被放大，其中无碰撞导航需要在严格的实时约束下找到一组狭窄的可行路径。 为了解决这个问题，我们提出了一种将条件流匹配（ CFM ）与模型预测路径积分（ MPPI ）控制相结合的导航框架，用于学习前向动力学模型（ FDM ）。 CFM模型缓解了MPPI的初始化问题并加速了其收敛，而采用FDM的MPPI通过迭代细化恢复了CFM模型训练分布之外的可行解。 我们将该框架集成到RAIBO-Wagon的全栈导航系统中， RAIBO-Wagon是一款四足拖曳定制旅行车，并在15米长的密闭空间中进行真实的户外实验，所有传感和计算都完全在船上进行。 该系统在40次试验中取得了85%的成功率，总负荷高达40公斤，几乎是RAIBO体重的100%。

## 3. Seeing and Imagining as One: Unified Video Generation and 4D Perception for Embodied World Modeling / 视觉和想象为一体：统一视频生成和4D感知实现实体世界建模

**Date**: 2026-10-29 | **arXiv**: [2610.39232v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.39232v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.39232v1)

**Categories**: cs.CV, cs.RO

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Embodied world models should not only imagine plausible futures, but also perceive the geometric, semantic, and motion structure of the evolving world. We present GenSight, a unified embodied world model that brings future video generation and dense perception into a single pretrained video diffusion transformer. GenSight uses a unified spatiotemporal representation for video, depth, embodiment segmentation, and first-frame-relative 2D flow. Built on a shared DiT backbone, GenSight uses lightweight task modulation to predict all modalities within the same architecture. All tasks are jointly optimized within a unified velocity prediction space. At inference, GenSight supports two complementary modes: multi-step future video generation with aligned dense predictions and single-step dense perception from an observed video. By unifying modality representation, model architecture, and prediction space, GenSight avoids separate modality-specific pipelines and couples appearance, geometry, semantics, and motion in a shared representation. It promotes cross-modal consistency and allows dense perception to benefit from the spatiotemporal priors learned through video generation. Trained on embodied manipulation data spanning multiple robot embodiments and human-hand interactions, GenSight demonstrates strong performance in both video generation and dense 4D perception, highlighting the effectiveness of unifying seeing and imagining for embodied world modeling.

具体化的世界模型不仅要想象合理的未来，还要感知进化世界的几何、语义和运动结构。 我们展示了GenSight ，这是一个统一的体现世界模型，将未来的视频生成和密集感知引入到单个预先训练的视频扩散变压器中。 GenSight对视频、深度、实施例分割和第一帧相对2D流使用统一的时空表示。 GenSight构建在共享的DiT骨干网上，使用轻量级任务调制来预测同一架构内的所有模式。 所有任务在统一的速度预测空间内共同优化。 在推断时， GenSight支持两种互补模式：具有对齐密集预测的多步未来视频生成和来自观察视频的单步密集感知。 通过统一模态表示、模型架构和预测空间， GenSight避免了单独的模态特定流水线，并在共享表示中耦合了外观、几何、语义和运动。 它促进了跨模态的一致性，并允许密集的感知从视频生成中学到的时空先验中受益。 GenSight经过跨越多个机器人实施例和人手交互的具体操作数据的训练，在视频生成和密集的4D感知方面表现出强大的性能，突出了统一视觉和想象以实现具体世界建模的有效性。

## 4. Generating Critic-Guided Synthetic Experience in Latent World Models / 在潜在世界模型中生成批评家引导的综合体验

**Date**: 2026-10-29 | **arXiv**: [2610.39100v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.39100v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.39100v1)

**Categories**: cs.LG, cs.AI

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Learning from offline datasets is limited in scalability, as collecting new experience is often costly or unsafe in the real world. World models offer an alternative way to generate new experience through a learned dynamics model, yet learning policies in latent space is limited due to compounding errors in imagined rollouts. To this end, we propose using the world model as a local transition generator, leveraging critic guidance to choose actions inside that model to generate synthetic experiences. We study action refinement methodologies, introducing Critic-Guided Synthetic Experience (CGSE) generation in world models. Specifically, CGSE augments an existing offline dataset by sampling candidate actions using an offline policy and refining each to maximize a first-order approximation of the $Q$-function within a trust region. We then use a frozen latent world model to imagine the future state given each updated action, and use the critic to choose the best one, appending this synthetic transition to the replay buffer. Our method requires no additional real-environment interaction or test-time planner, as it generates synthetic experience offline using the world model. On three OGBench manipulation tasks, CGSE improves an offline policy by a relative improvement of $7.1\%$ on the average success rate.

从离线数据集中学习的可扩展性有限，因为在现实世界中收集新体验通常成本高昂或不安全。 世界模型提供了一种通过学习动态模型生成新体验的替代方法，但由于想象中的部署中的复合错误，潜在空间中的学习策略受到限制。 为此，我们建议使用世界模型作为本地转换生成器，利用批评者指导来选择模型中的操作以生成综合体验。 我们研究动作细化方法，在世界模型中引入批评引导合成体验（ CGSE ）生成。 具体而言， CGSE通过使用离线策略对候选操作进行采样并优化每个操作来扩充现有离线数据集，以最大化信任区域内$ Q $ -函数的一阶近似。 然后，我们使用冻结的潜在世界模型来想象给定每个更新动作的未来状态，并使用批评者选择最佳状态，将这种合成转换附加到重播缓冲区。 我们的方法不需要额外的真实环境交互或测试时间规划器，因为它使用世界模型离线生成合成体验。 在三项OGBench操作任务中， CGSE相对于平均成功率提高了$ 7.1\ % $ ，从而改进了离线策略。

</details>

## 5. Tactus: Tactile & Pose Sensing Glove for Natural Dexterity Data Collection / Tactus ：触觉和姿势感应手套，用于天然灵巧数据收集

**Date**: 2026-10-29 | **arXiv**: [2610.39076v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.39076v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.39076v1)

**Categories**: cs.RO, cs.AI, cs.AR, cs.LG

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Collecting naturalistic human manipulation demonstrations with accurate contact force and hand pose is essential for robot dexterous manipulation policy and foundational world model learning - yet no existing wearable platform provides both calibrated fingertip force sensing and occlusion-resilient hand tracking in a form factor that preserves natural manipulation behavior. We present Tactus, a textile-integrated sensing glove that co-optimizes three design pillars: (1) a thin, conformal 3D-knit form factor (1.5 mm fingertip stackup) designed to minimize interference with natural manipulation, (2) strategically placed capacitive tactile sensors at manipulation-relevant regions informed by empirical contact studies, and (3) IMU-fused pose tracking that maintains accuracy through the hand-object occlusions where manipulation data is most valuable. ## 6. Certified Access: Test-Time Compute on Conservative Physics-Structured AI Memory / 认证访问：保守物理结构化AI内存上的测试时间计算

**Date**: 2026-10-29 | **arXiv**: [2610.39073v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.39073v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.39073v1)

**Categories**: cs.AI, hep-ph

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Test-time compute mechanisms, such as retries, confidence-based escalation and non-local routing, are conventionally deployed as black-box additions to trained models. They typically add forward passes or halting heads, but give no structural account of what the extra compute buys or what it costs against the model's stability guarantees. We therefore propose a theoretical and empirical framework for test-time compute on a Latent Dynamics Model used as the memory. When an encoder's latent state $(q,p)$ is advanced by a damped symplectic (velocity-Verlet) step of a learned Hamiltonian, every mechanism that expands access has an exact phase-volume Jacobian, a bounded energy change and an exact latch-transport law. We split reachability failures into escape failures and reach failures. A Lorentz squeeze resolves escape failures by injecting bounded, re-absorbable energy. A gated canonical translation (a one-step channel across $C_T$) resolves reach failures at $\detJ=1$ exactly. We verify this certificate stack on a designed analytic testbed. Reaching just beyond $C_T$ with the squeeze requires energy that grows quadratically in the excess distance. The gated channel's energy cost equals the potential difference between its endpoints, which is zero on the matched channel measured here, and so does not grow with distance in this testbed. An untrained state-replacing map annihilates phase volume and erases stored charge, while the gated channel transports it. On a learned physics-structured memory, we show calibrated compute rationing and evaluate the scaling limits of non-local routing against a physics-free baseline. The main contribution is the formalization and verification of the certificate stack, not benchmark performance.

测试时间计算机制，如重试、基于置信度的升级和非本地路由，通常作为黑盒添加到训练模型中。 它们通常添加前向传递或停止头，但没有给出额外计算购买的内容或与模型的稳定性保证相比的成本的结构性说明。 因此，我们提出了一个理论和经验框架，用于在用作存储器的潜在动力学模型上进行测试时间计算。 当编码器的潜态$ (q, p) $通过学习的哈密顿函数的阻尼辛（速度-小波）步长提前时，扩展访问的每个机制都具有精确的相位体积雅可比、有界能量变化和精确的闩锁传输定律。 我们将可达性故障分为转义故障和REACH故障。 洛仑兹挤压通过注入有界的可再吸收能量来解决逃逸故障。 门控规范翻译（跨$ C_T $的一步通道）完全解决了$\ detJ = 1 $的REACH故障。 我们在设计的分析测试平台上验证此证书堆栈。 挤压刚好超过$ C_T $ ，需要的能量在多余的距离内平方增长。 门控通道的能量成本等于其端点之间的潜在差异，在此处测量的匹配通道上为零，因此在此测试平台中不会随距离增长。 未经训练的状态替换映射湮灭相位体积并擦除存储的电荷，同时门控通道传输它。

</details>

## 7. Context Compaction for World Models / 世界模型的上下文压缩

**Date**: 2026-10-28 | **arXiv**: [2610.38980v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.38980v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.38980v1)

**Categories**: cs.AI

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

World models let observers explore and interact with generated worlds. Recent work shows that when observers revisit a region, current models often fail to regenerate it consistently. We view this as a context retrieval problem. LLM agents manage context retrieval at two layers of their stack. At the model layer, the KV cache design determines how much context fits in memory. At the agent harness layer, harnesses such as Claude Code pick the context sent to the LLM. Prior work on world models has mostly focused only on the model layer. In this paper, we make three contributions. First, we build a benchmark to measure world model context retrieval. It generates new worlds for every episode, preventing a model from recalling rooms using training data. It also separates context retrieval from guesses based on nearby frames. Second, we build an agent harness that performs context compaction for world models, which to our knowledge no prior work has studied. Third, we use our benchmark to study four world model designs. Context compaction lets our world models retrieve rooms up to 4 times beyond their context window without retraining. However, world models with sliding-window attention lose most of their context retrieval when dreaming. Therefore, a dreaming world agent benefits more from extending the reach of its world model using context compaction than switching to sliding window attention at the model layer.

世界模型允许观察者探索生成的世界并与之交互。 最近的研究表明，当观察者重新审视一个地区时，当前的模型往往无法始终如一地再生它。 我们认为这是一个上下文检索问题。 LLM代理在其堆栈的两层管理上下文检索。 在模型层， KV缓存设计确定内存中的上下文容量。 在客服代表线束层， Claude Code等线束选择发送给LLM的上下文。 之前关于世界模型的工作主要集中在模型层。 在本文中，我们做出了三点贡献。 首先，我们建立了一个基准来衡量世界模型上下文检索。 它为每个剧集生成新的世界，防止模型使用训练数据召回房间。 它还将上下文检索与基于附近帧的猜测分开。 其次，我们构建了一个为世界模型执行上下文压缩的代理线束，据我们所知，以前的工作还没有研究过。 第三，我们使用我们的基准来研究四个世界模型设计。 上下文压缩使我们的世界模型能够在上下文窗口之外检索房间多达4次，而无需重新训练。 然而，具有滑动窗口注意力的世界模型在做梦时失去了大部分的上下文检索。 因此，使用上下文压缩扩展其世界模型的范围比在模型层切换到滑动窗口关注更有益。

## 8. Structural-Test Geometry as an Inductive Bias for Partially Observable World Models / 结构测试几何作为部分可观测世界模型的归纳偏差

**Date**: 2026-10-28 | **arXiv**: [2610.38903v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.38903v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.38903v1)

**Categories**: cs.LG

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Predictive world-model objectives need not organize latent histories according to distinctions required for later control. With privileged simulator access during training, should response-derived structural information be predicted, decoded, or imposed directly on latent geometry--and how do these choices compare with structured belief distillation? We define posterior-marginalized structural tests whose response distributions induce a pseudometric over histories, then train quotient-preserving (QP) world models by aligning shared latent Euclidean distances to that metric. Across a fixed six-task diagnostic suite, the largest differences appear under held-out structural variation: QP improves planning success by 26.1 points $[21.5,30.7]$ over metric decoding and 6.4 points $[3.8,9.0]$ over structured belief distillation. On GridWorld, metric decoding accurately recovers held-out structural distances ($ρ=.91$) while its native latent geometry remains more distorted than QP's (paired IQD difference $.22$ $[.20,.24]$), showing that relational recoverability and direct geometric alignment can diverge.

预测世界模型目标不需要根据后期控制所需的区别来组织潜在的历史。 在训练期间使用特权模拟器访问时，是否应该预测、解码或直接将响应衍生的结构信息强加于潜在几何体--这些选择与结构化信念提取相比如何？ 我们定义了后边缘化结构检验，其响应分布诱导历史上的伪度量，然后通过将共享的潜在欧几里得距离与该度量对齐来训练商保留（ QP ）世界模型。 在固定的六任务诊断套件中，最大的差异出现在保持不变的结构变化下： QP使规划成功率比指标解码提高了26.1个百分点[21.5,30.7]美元，比结构化信念提取提高了6.4个百分点[3.8,9.0]美元。 在GridWorld上，度量解码准确地恢复了伸出的结构距离（ $ ρ = .91 $ ） ，而其原生隐含几何形状仍然比QP的更加扭曲（成对IQD差值$ .22 $ $ [.20, .24] $ ） ，这表明关系可恢复性和直接几何对齐可以发散。
