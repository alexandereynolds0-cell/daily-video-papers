## 1. Posterior Matching Distillation / 后验匹配蒸馏

**Date**: 2026-11-19 | **arXiv**: [2611.26629v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26629v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.26629v1)

**Categories**: cs.CV

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Diffusion distillation accelerates image and video generation by compressing a pretrained teacher's iterative sampling process into a few network evaluations. Distribution-based methods achieve this by matching the student's output distribution to the teacher's. This matching is often driven by reverse-KL objectives, whose mode-seeking bias favors dominant teacher modes at the expense of diversity across samples. We introduce Posterior Matching Distillation (PMD), a new member of the distribution-based distillation family that combines mode-covering posterior sampling with any-step generation. At zero population loss, PMD recovers the teacher's full posterior of clean images at each trained noise level. Its stochastic student supports any-step sampling budgets within the trained timestep grid without further training. To learn these posteriors, PMD matches exponentially reweighted averages of student samples conditioned on a fixed noisy state to corresponding teacher posterior means. Under Gaussian corruption, exponentially tilted posterior mean is a single teacher evaluation at a shifted input. We prove that, matching tilted means over an open neighborhood uniquely identifies the full posterior. PMD thus supervises the entire conditional distribution through a family of mean constraints, without any auxiliary student-score network. Experiments on SDXL and FLUX.2 [klein] 4B demonstrate that PMD achieves the best FID among 4-step methods on both models, while maintaining competitive or leading performance across prompt-alignment and perceptual-quality metrics.

## 2. One Prior, Two Dynamics: Repurposing Video Diffusion Transformers for Self-Supervised Latent Action Learning / 一个先验，两个动态：将视频扩散变压器用于自监督潜在动作学习

**Date**: 2026-11-19 | **arXiv**: [2611.26617v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26617v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.26617v1)

**Categories**: cs.CV, cs.AI, cs.LG, cs.RO

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Videos reveal how the world changes, but rarely the actions that cause those changes. Latent action learning recovers such supervision from visual transitions, yet often learns the underlying dynamics largely from scratch. We ask whether a pretrained video generator can instead provide the prior for both inverse and forward dynamics. We introduce O$^{2}$LaF, built on a simple principle: one prior, two dynamics. We initialize both models from the same pretrained video DiT and adapt them to their distinct roles: a feed-forward transition encoder with learnable action queries for inverse dynamics, and a causal, action-conditioned video generator for forward dynamics. By controlling information access and optimizing both dynamics end-to-end through a compact bottleneck, O$^{2}$LaF learns transferable actions from unlabeled videos. Across navigation and robotic manipulation, O$^{2}$LaF sets a new state of the art in few-shot action probing and enables stronger zero-shot action transfer across visual contexts and embodiments. Beyond generative video priors, we further extend our framework to predictive representation priors such as V-JEPA2, achieving competitive performance. Our findings suggest a broader paradigm for latent-action learning: repurposing pretrained video models to discover transferable actions and predict their consequences.

视频揭示了世界是如何变化的，但很少揭示导致这些变化的动作。潜在动作学习从视觉转换中恢复这种监督，但通常是从零开始学习潜在的动力学。我们提出一个问题：预训练的视频生成器是否可以为逆动力学和正动力学提供先验信息。我们介绍了 O$^{2}$LaF，它建立在一个简单的原理之上：一个先验，两种动力学。我们从同一个预训练的视频DiT初始化这两个模型，并使它们适应各自不同的角色：一个具有可学习动作查询的前馈式转换编码器，用于逆向动力学；一个因果性、以动作为条件的视频生成器，用于正向动力学。通过控制信息访问，并通过一个紧凑的瓶颈对两种动态进行端到端优化，O$^{2}$LaF能够从未标记的视频中学习可迁移的动作。在导航和机器人操纵方面，O$^{2}$LaF 在少样本动作探测方面树立了新的标杆，并实现了跨视觉环境和具身形态的更强大的零样本动作迁移。除了生成式视频先验之外，我们还进一步将我们的框架扩展到预测性表示先验（如V-JEPA2 ），实现了具有竞争力的性能。我们的研究结果提出了一种更广泛的潜在动作学习范式：将预训练的视频模型重新用于发现可迁移的动作并预测其后果。

## 5. FFGaussType: Feed-Forward Conversion of Glyph and SVG Geometry into Compact Gaussian Assets / FFGaussType：将字形和 SVG 几何图形前馈转换为紧凑的高斯资产

**Date**: 2026-11-19 | **arXiv**: [2611.26431v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26431v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.26431v1)

**Categories**: cs.CV, cs.GR

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Immersive 3D content allows text and graphical elements, such as subtitles, annotations, and icons, to be placed inside a scene rather than displayed as flat overlays. Placed this way, they respect occlusion and perspective and take on user-assigned materials and lighting while preserving their original shapes. In Gaussian scenes, however, each new glyph or icon requires its own iterative optimization, which limits scalable content creation. We present FFGaussType, which converts extruded glyph and SVG outlines into Gaussian assets in a single forward pass. A point encoder maps anchors sampled on the known surface to a single feature plane, from which a decoder shared by all assets predicts neural Gaussians carrying surface normals. This separates geometric conversion from material and lighting choices, and the feature plane supports image and video coding for compact delivery. We construct a paired dataset of 27,221 glyph and SVG icon assets with anchors and calibrated normal and silhouette renderings, holding out font families for evaluation. Conversion from prepared anchors takes 9.1 ms per asset, at a fraction of the compute and memory of prior baselines. On unseen font families and test icons, FFGaussType achieves $1.62^\circ$ normal error and $0.985$ silhouette IoU, the best among the compared feed-forward converters, matching the fidelity that per-asset fitting needs thousands of steps to reach. The code and dataset will be released after publication.

沉浸式 3D 内容允许将文本和图形元素（如字幕、注释和图标）放置在场景中，而不是以平面叠加的形式显示。以这种方式放置时，它们会遵循遮挡和透视效果，并采用用户指定的材质和光照，同时保留其原始形状。然而，在高斯场景中，每个新的字形或图标都需要进行单独的迭代优化，这限制了可扩展的内容创作。我们推出了 FFGaussType，它可以在一次前向传递中将拉伸的字形和 SVG 轮廓转换为高斯资产。点编码器将从已知表面采样的锚点映射到单个特征平面，所有资产共享的解码器从该特征平面预测携带表面法线的神经辐射场。这会将几何转换与材质和光照选择分离开来，并且特征平面支持图像和视频编码，以实现紧凑的交付。我们构建了一个包含 27,221 个字形和 SVG 图标资产的成对数据集，其中包含锚点以及校准后的法线和轮廓渲染，并保留了字体系列用于评估。从已准备好的锚点进行转换，每个资产需要 9.1 毫秒，仅需先前基准的一小部分计算和内存。在未见过的字体系列和测试图标上，FFGaussType实现了$1.62^\circ$的法线误差和$0.985$的轮廓IoU，这是所比较的前馈转换器中最好的结果，达到了每个资产拟合需要数千步才能达到的保真度。代码和数据集将在论文发表后发布。

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Current leading video generation models can synthesize highly realistic videos, while still frequently violating physical and logical constraints in multi-step reasoning and planning, even after training with large-scale reasoning data or existing diffusion RL methods. ## 34. ZERITH V2.5: From Predictive Power to Policy Learnability / ZERITH V2.5: From Predictive Power to Policy Learnability

**Date**: 2026-11-18 | **arXiv**: [2611.23746v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23746v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.23746v1)

**Categories**: cs.RO

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

When humans learn to manipulate an unfamiliar object, they may already be able to anticipate how its state will change while still being unable to control it skillfully. This gap between prediction and control motivates us to study how the priors acquired by video generation models can be used effectively by an action policy. We present ZERITH V2.5, a video-action model (VAM) in which the action policy reads intermediate features of a video generation model, and use it to study, under fixed demonstrations and a fixed total number of updates, what kind of predictive training improves subsequent action learning.   We find that the way video predictive training is supervised affects subsequent action learning. Initial loss and gradient measurements at the start of joint training indicate that target-domain predictive training gives the video model a better starting state, making subsequent joint learning of prediction and action easier. Under matched data and training budgets, predictive adaptation improves control. We evaluate the complete policy on LIBERO, LIBERO-Plus, Meta-World MT50, and real-robot tasks.
