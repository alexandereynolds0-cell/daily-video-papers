## 1. E-MAPS: Event-Based Promptable Video Segmentation with Memory-as-Prompt / E-MAPS：基于事件的提示视频分割，内存即提示

**Date**: 2026-11-24 | **arXiv**: [2611.33017v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33017v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.33017v1)

**Categories**: cs.CV

**Code**: https://github.com/adonaydem/emaps-wacv

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

RGB-based video object segmentation (VOS) remains brittle under fast motion and adverse illumination. Event cameras preserve signal under blur and challenging exposure, but provide little evidence when motion slows. Interaction can restore object state for subsequent propagation, yet promptable event methods merely address single windows. We formalize event-based Promptable Video Segmentation (ePVS), where prompts at arbitrary windows initialize, correct, or introduce objects and predictions. We present Event Memory-As-Prompt Segmentation (E-MAPS), which transfers a pretrained promptable segmenter to event streams and learns propagation and correction. Event-to-Token (E2T) aligns recurrent event features with pretrained representations while preserving prompt-conditioning priors. Memory-Aware Prompting (MAP) converts object-conditioned history into sparse prompts, allowing memory and user inputs to share one decoder pathway efficiently. We construct Event-View Masklets (EViM) from 206K aligned RGB-event pairs and 620K masks for two-stage representation transfer and native event-video learning, and establish three training-disjoint benchmarks. E-MAPS leads prompt-once and interactive ePVS across all three benchmarks while being more efficient. It further demonstrates strong performance on zero-shot object tracking benchmarks. Project page: https://github.com/adonaydem/emaps-wacv

基于 RGB 的视频对象分割 (VOS) 在快速运动和不利照明下仍然很脆弱。事件摄像机在模糊和具有挑战性的曝光下保留信号，但在运动减慢时提供很少的证据。交互可以恢复对象状态以供后续传播，但提示事件方法仅处理单个窗口。我们将基于事件的提示视频分割（ePVS）形式化，其中任意窗口的提示可以初始化、纠正或引入对象和预测。我们提出了事件记忆提示分段（E-MAPS），它将预训练的提示分段器传输到事件流并学习传播和校正。事件到令牌（E2T）将重复事件特征与预训练表示相结合，同时保留提示调节先验。内存感知提示 (MAP) 将对象条件历史记录转换为稀疏提示，允许内存和用户输入有效地共享一个解码器路径。我们从 206K 对齐的 RGB 事件对和 620K 掩模构建事件视图掩模 (EViM)，用于两阶段表示传输和本机事件视频学习，并建立三个训练不相交基准。 E-MAPS 在所有三个基准测试中领先于一次​​性提示和交互式 ePVS，同时效率更高。它进一步展示了零样本对象跟踪基准测试的强大性能。项目页面：https://github.com/adonaydem/emaps-wacv

## 2. RePix-VFI: Re-rendering Observed Pixels for Video Frame Interpolation with Generative Priors from One-Step Diffusion / RePix-VFI：使用一步扩散的生成先验重新渲染视频帧插值的观察像素

**Date**: 2026-11-24 | **arXiv**: [2611.33016v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33016v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.33016v1)

**Categories**: cs.CV

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Multi-frame video frame interpolation (VFI) aims to reconstruct dense video from sparse keyframes, requiring accurate intermediate motion and faithful appearance. As temporal gaps grow, flow-based methods can retain observed textures but misplace them under ambiguous motion, whereas iterative latent-diffusion models can synthesize plausible content but incur substantial sampling costs and may alter details during reconstruction with a variational autoencoder (VAE). We present RePix-VFI, named for Re-rendering observed Pixels, a multi-frame VFI framework that couples draft-conditioned one-step video diffusion with reconstruction from observed keyframes. A pretrained video diffusion model refines a flow-rendered draft of the whole window in a single forward pass. For each target frame, our generative readback matches its two bounding keyframes to the generated prediction using an optical-flow estimator. The resulting correspondences guide re-rendering of original keyframe RGB pixels outside the VAE. A lightweight appearance fusion head combines these renderings with generated appearance. Experiments on benchmark datasets demonstrate significant improvements in reconstruction and perceptual quality. On SNU-FILM at $\times$8 and $\times$16, RePix-VFI achieves the highest PSNR and the lowest LPIPS, FloLPIPS, and tOF among the compared methods, exceeding the best evaluated diffusion-based baseline by 1.71 and 1.48dB in PSNR, respectively. It also runs 32--42$\times$ faster than the state-of-the-art multi-frame VFI diffusion model.

多帧视频帧插值（VFI）旨在从稀疏关键帧重建密集视频，需要准确的中间运动和忠实的外观。随着时间间隙的增长，基于流的方法可以保留观察到的纹理，但在模糊运动下会错位它们，而迭代潜在扩散模型可以合成合理的内容，但会产生大量的采样成本，并且可能会在使用变分自动编码器（VAE）重建期间改变细节。我们提出了 RePix-VFI，以重新渲染观察到​​的像素命名，这是一种多帧 VFI 框架，它将草图调节的单步视频扩散与观察到的关键帧的重建结合起来。预训练的视频扩散模型在一次前向传递中细化了整个窗口的流渲染草稿。对于每个目标帧，我们的生成读回使用光流估计器将其两个边界关键帧与生成的预测进行匹配。由此产生的对应关系指导 VAE 外部原始关键帧 RGB 像素的重新渲染。轻量级外观融合头将这些渲染与生成的外观相结合。基准数据集上的实验证明了重建和感知质量的显着改进。在 $\times$8 和 $\times$16 的 SNU-FILM 上，RePix-VFI 在比较方法中实现了最高的 PSNR 和最低的 LPIPS、FloLPIPS 和 tOF，PSNR 分别超出最佳评估的基于扩散的基线 1.71 和 1.48dB。它的运行速度也比最先进的多帧 VFI 扩散模型快 32--42$\times$。

</details>

## 3. PAVE: Preference-Aligned Visual Egocentric Learning for the Blind and Low-Vision Users / PAVE：为盲人和低视力用户提供偏好一致的视觉自我中心学习

**Date**: 2026-11-24 | **arXiv**: [2611.32996v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32996v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.32996v1)

**Categories**: cs.CV

**Code**: https://github.com/shawnliang420/PAVE.

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Egocentric video question answering enables blind and low-vision (BLV) users to query their immediate surroundings through natural language. However, existing benchmarks and models primarily optimize for semantic agreement with reference answers, reducing visual assistance to a purely factual matching task. In real-world scenarios, a factually correct response remains practically unhelpful if it lacks the spatial orientation, actionable detail, or conciseness required by the immediate task of the user. Drawing upon real BLV egocentric videos and authentic user queries from EgoBlind, we construct the PA-EgoQA, a preference-aligned dataset that reconstructs reference answers according to empirical BLV communication criteria. Based on this dataset, we introduce Preference-Aligned Visual Egocentric Learning (PAVE), a unified learning and inference framework that couples structured answer-policy fine-tuning with deterministic question-conditioned routing to adapt response formulation directly to the surface form of the query at inference time. We further align the model via Filter-GRPO, which pairs a hierarchical correctness-quality reward with contrast-based candidate-group filtering to extract learning signals from both factual accuracy contrasts and fine-grained quality variations among correct answers. Extensive experiments across three vision-language backbones demonstrate consistent improvements in factual accuracy alongside substantial gains in assistive response quality. These results establish a concrete paradigm for translating human-centered response requirements into egocentric supervision, generation policies, and post-training objectives. Code will be provided at https://github.com/shawnliang420/PAVE.

以自我为中心的视频问答使盲人和低视力 (BLV) 用户能够通过自然语言查询他们周围的环境。然而，现有的基准和模型主要针对与参考答案的语义一致性进行优化，从而减少了对纯粹事实匹配任务的视觉辅助。在现实场景中，如果缺乏用户当前任务所需的空间方向、可操作的细节或简洁性，那么事实上正确的响应实际上仍然没有帮助。利用真实的 BLV 以自我为中心的视频和来自 EgoBlind 的真实用户查询，我们构建了 PA-EgoQA，这是一个偏好一致的数据集，可根据经验 BLV 通信标准重建参考答案。基于该数据集，我们引入了偏好对齐视觉自我中心学习（PAVE），这是一个统一的学习和推理框架，它将结构化答案策略微调与确定性问题条件路由相结合，以在推理时将响应公式直接适应查询的表面形式。我们通过 Filter-GRPO 进一步调整模型，它将分层正确性质量奖励与基于对比的候选组过滤配对，以从事实准确性对比和正确答案之间的细粒度质量变化中提取学习信号。跨三个视觉语言主干的广泛实验表明，事实准确性不断提高，辅助响应质量也显着提高。这些结果建立了一个具体的范式，用于将以人为中心的响应要求转化为以自我为中心的监督、生成政策和培训后目标。代码将在 https://github.com/shawnliang420/PAVE 提供。

</details>

## 4. Teaching Time Without Losing General Retrieval: Adapting Multimodal Embeddings for Time-Sensitive Video Retrieval / 在不损失一般检索的情况下教授时间：采用多模态嵌入进行时间敏感的视频检索

**Date**: 2026-11-24 | **arXiv**: [2611.32971v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32971v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.32971v1)

**Categories**: cs.CV

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

In text-to-video retrieval, MLLM-based embedding models do well when the text describes the scene (general retrieval), but poorly when it describes actions and their direction (time-sensitive retrieval). TARA, a pioneering work, fine-tunes such a model on text triplets with temporal hard negatives; it improves time-sensitive retrieval but lowers general retrieval (mean R@1 over six benchmarks in our rerun on Qwen3-VL-Embedding-8B: 58.4->44.8), and its released model keeps general retrieval only when ensembled with Qwen3-VL-Embedding. Our goal is strong general and time-sensitive retrieval with one model and one vector per clip. Three findings lead to three simple components. Keep loss: general retrieval follows how far the output embeddings drift from the base model, not how far the weights move; we therefore keep them near the base model, which keeps most general retrieval even without general-domain data. Pair loss: the text embeddings of an action description and its reversed-direction version are well separated, but the video embeddings of a clip and its reversed copy are close; we therefore widen the gap between the video embeddings of each clip and its reversed copy, in the direction of the difference between their two text embeddings. Middle-layer LoRA: frames are combined in the middle layers, and temporal information peaks in or just after them; we therefore train LoRA only on these layers, which nearly matches training all weights on most time-sensitive benchmarks with 0.15% of the parameters and keeps general retrieval better. General R@1 stays at 58.4, while R@1 with each clip's reversed copy in the gallery rises from 55.5 to 63.2; the gains carry over to unseen benchmarks and InternVL2-8B-RA. The keep loss is also a remedy for the TARA recipe (general R@1: 44.8->57.8), and ensembling TARA with Ours gives the highest scores on RTime and unseen CiA among the compared models.

在文本到视频检索中，基于 MLLM 的嵌入模型在文本描述场景（一般检索）时表现良好，但在描述动作及其方向（时间敏感检索）时表现不佳。 TARA 是一项开创性的工作，它对带有时间硬否定的文本三元组的模型进行了微调；它改进了时间敏感检索，但降低了一般检索（在 Qwen3-VL-Embedding-8B 上重新运行时，六个基准的平均 R@1：58.4->44.8），并且其发布的模型仅在与 Qwen3-VL-Embedding 集成时才保留一般检索。我们的目标是通过每个剪辑一个模型和一个向量实现强大的通用且时间敏感的检索。三项发现引出了三个简单的组成部分。保持损失：一般检索遵循输出嵌入偏离基本模型的距离，而不是权重移动的距离；因此，我们将它们保留在基本模型附近，即使没有通用领域数据，也可以保持大多数通用检索。 Pair loss：动作描述的文本嵌入与其反向版本分离得很好，但剪辑的视频嵌入与其反向副本很接近；因此，我们在两个文本嵌入之间的差异方向上扩大了每个剪辑的视频嵌入与其反向副本之间的差距。中间层LoRA：帧在中间层组合，时间信息在中间层或之后达到峰值；因此，我们仅在这些层上训练 LoRA，这几乎与使用 0.15% 的参数在大多数时间敏感基准上训练所有权重相匹配，并且可以更好地保持一般检索。一般R@1保持在58.4，而画廊中每个剪辑的反转副本的R@1从55.5上升到63.2；收益会延续到未见过的基准测试和 InternVL2-8B-RA。保持损失也是对 TARA 配方的一种补救措施（一般 R@1：44.8->57.8），并且将 TARA 与 Ours 相结合在比较模型中在 RTime 和 unseen CiA 上给出了最高分。

</details>

## 5. OmniHarness-RSI: Modality-Aware Harness Evolution for Omnimodal Understanding / OmniHarness-RSI：用于全模态理解的模态感知线束演化

**Date**: 2026-11-24 | **arXiv**: [2611.32951v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32951v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.32951v1)

**Categories**: cs.CV

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Recent advances in harness-based recursive self-improvement (RSI) have improved video understanding by evolving executable agent workflows without updating foundation model parameters. However, extending this paradigm to omnimodal understanding remains challenging, as the heterogeneous and interdependent nature of multimodal perception and reasoning creates a complex evolution space, making targeted harness optimization difficult. To address these challenges, we propose OmniHarness-RSI, a modality-aware framework for evolving agent harnesses. Specifically, we factorize the evolution space into three capability dimensions: modality routing and collaboration, modality-aware evidence localization, and modality-specific perception. Based on this factorization, we construct a Factorized-Harness Agent integrating specialized perception experts and audio-video retrieval tools. We further introduce modality-guided harness evolution, which identifies deficiencies through structured modality-capability attribution and refines corresponding harness strategies through interactive code revisions and execution feedback. Validation-guided selection and held-out evaluation assess generalization within and across datasets. Experiments on WorldSense, Daily-Omni, and OmniVideoBench demonstrate test accuracy improvements of 8.86, 8.13, and 6.23 percentage points, respectively, consistently outperforming modality-agnostic RSI. The evolved harnesses also demonstrate cross-dataset transfer without adaptation, while reducing planner calls and viewed frames.

基于线束的递归自我改进 (RSI) 的最新进展通过改进可执行代理工作流程而无需更新基础模型参数，从而提高了视频理解。然而，将这种范式扩展到全模态理解仍然具有挑战性，因为多模态感知和推理的异构和相互依赖的性质创造了复杂的进化空间，使得有针对性的线束优化变得困难。为了应对这些挑战，我们提出了 OmniHarness-RSI，这是一种用于不断发展的代理线束的模态感知框架。具体来说，我们将演化空间分解为三个能力维度：模态路由和协作、模态感知证据本地化和模态特定感知。基于这种分解，我们构建了一个集成专业感知专家和音视频检索工具的分解线束代理。我们进一步引入了模态引导的线束演化，它通过结构化模态能力归因来识别缺陷，并通过交互式代码修订和执行反馈来完善相应的线束策略。验证引导的选择和保留的评估评估数据集内和数据集之间的泛化。 WorldSense、Daily-Omni 和 OmniVideoBench 上的实验表明，测试准确度分别提高了 8.86、8.13 和 6.23 个百分点，始终优于与模态无关的 RSI。进化后的工具还展示了无需适应的跨数据集传输，同时减少了规划器调用和查看的帧。

</details>

## 6. Efficient Video Retrieval Augmented Generation via Token Allocation / 通过代币分配高效视频检索增强生成

**Date**: 2026-11-24 | **arXiv**: [2611.32938v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32938v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.32938v1)

**Categories**: cs.CV, cs.AI, cs.LG, eess.IV

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Efficient long-video understanding requires identifying relevant evidence while minimizing the tokens processed by vision-language models (VLMs). Existing video retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) methods rely on frame selection and token compression, but overlook how much each additional token is worth. We introduce Lembas, a plug-and-play framework that formulates video RAG as a token allocation problem over frames and subtitles under a shared budget. Our key insight is that the value of a token diminishes within each item and decreases further as similar evidence receives more tokens. Lembas captures both effects in a single objective and solves it with an efficient iterative water-filling algorithm that has a provable convergence guarantee. For multi-turn conversations, Lembas reuses the tokens processed in earlier turns and refines relevance with the VLM's attention at no extra cost. Our evaluations show that Lembas improves accuracy by up to 7.2 percentage points over state-of-the-art baselines at comparable compute. On multi-turn video understanding, Lembas achieves up to 1.5 percentage points higher accuracy with 1.7-17.3x fewer TFLOPs.

高效的长视频理解需要识别相关证据，同时最大限度地减少视觉语言模型 (VLM) 处理的标记。现有的视频检索增强生成（RAG）方法依赖于帧选择和令牌压缩，但忽略了每个附加令牌的价值。我们介绍 Lembas，一个即插即用的框架，它将视频 RAG 表述为共享预算下帧和字幕的令牌分配问题。我们的主要见解是，每个项目中代币的价值都会递减，并且随着类似的证据收到更多代币，代币的价值会进一步降低。 Lembas 在单个目标中捕获了这两种效应，并使用高效的迭代注水算法来解决它，该算法具有可证明的收敛保证。对于多轮对话，Lembas 重用在前轮中处理的令牌，并完善与 VLM 注意力的相关性，无需额外成本。我们的评估表明，在可比计算下，Lembas 的准确率比最先进的基线提高了 7.2 个百分点。在多轮视频理解方面，Lembas 的准确度提高了 1.5 个百分点，TFLOP 减少了 1.7-17.3 倍。

## 7. PhysMend: Verification-Guided Program Refinement for Physics-Aware Video Generation / PhysMend：用于物理感知视频生成的验证引导程序细化

**Date**: 2026-11-24 | **arXiv**: [2611.32821v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32821v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.32821v1)

**Categories**: cs.CV

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Video generative models synthesize realistic scenes, yet their interactions often violate physics. A ball dropped onto sand should displace grains and settle into a crater, whereas a generated video may show it passing through the surface or leaving the sand flat. Post-hoc methods typically score generated videos with a vision-language model (VLM), but such scores offer limited guidance on which object or parameter to modify, and each correction costs another synthesis. Simulation-based pipelines instead run fixed stages before synthesis, so simulation errors reach the video. To address these limitations, we present PhysMend, a training-free framework for verification-guided program refinement. PhysMend describes the target event as an editable physical program of grounded objects, events, and required relations, paired with tunable execution parameters. Our key insight is to verify and repair the event on its simulated rollout and on the depth video that conditions the generator (depth control) rather than on generated videos, enabling quantitative violation measurement, targeted editing, and fast re-verification before synthesis. A hybrid verifier evaluates semantic event consistency, physical plausibility, solver stability, and visibility in the depth control. VLM judgments are confined to event semantics and cannot override failed physical measurements, which limits the impact of VLM hallucinations. Each failed check localizes the program fields eligible for repair, and its measured violation scales a bounded update of their parameters. Only accepted depth control reaches the generator, which runs once per input. Experiments on the commonly used benchmarks Physics-IQ, PhyGenBench, and VBench-2.0 demonstrate substantial gains across different generator backbones; for example, PhysMend raises the overall Physics-IQ score of Wan-VACE from 27.33 to 39.40.

视频生成模型合成现实场景，但它们的交互通常违反物理原理。掉落到沙子上的球应该会取代颗粒并落入火山口，而生成的视频可能会显示球穿过表面或使沙子保持平坦。事后方法通常使用视觉语言模型 (VLM) 对生成的视频进行评分，但此类评分对于修改哪个对象或参数提供的指导有限，而且每次校正都需要进行另一次合成。基于模拟的管道在合成之前运行固定阶段，因此模拟错误会到达视频。为了解决这些限制，我们提出了 PhysMend，这是一个无需培训的框架，用于验证引导的程序细化。 PhysMend 将目标事件描述为由接地对象、事件和所需关系组成的可编辑物理程序，并配有可调执行参数。我们的主要见解是在模拟推出和调节生成器（深度控制）的深度视频上验证和修复事件，而不是在生成的视频上验证和修复事件，从而实现定量违规测量、有针对性的编辑以及合成前的快速重新验证。混合验证器评估语义事件一致性、物理合理性、求解器稳定性和深度控制中的可见性。 VLM判断仅限于事件语义，不能覆盖失败的物理测量，这限制了VLM幻觉的影响。每次失败的检查都会定位符合修复条件的程序字段，并且其测量的违规会缩放其参数的有限更新。只有接受的深度控制才会到达生成器，生成器每个输入运行一次。在常用基准测试Physics-IQ、PhyGenBench 和VBench-2.0 上进行的实验表明，不同的生成器主干具有显着的增益；例如，PhysMend 将 Wan-VACE 的物理智商总体得分从 27.33 提高到 39.40。

</details>

## 8. Residual Guidance for Real-Time Interactive Video Generation / 实时交互式视频生成的残差指导

**Date**: 2026-11-24 | **arXiv**: [2611.32820v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32820v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.32820v1)

**Categories**: cs.MM, cs.AI, cs.CV

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Real-time interactive video generation allows users to change the prompt during generation to steer the evolving video. However, existing streaming models, which generate videos chunk by chunk, often keep following the previous prompt after a switch. We trace this failure to context inertia, where the previous chunk acts as a strong prior that suppresses the new prompt. Furthermore, we find that this inertia persists even when the cache retains only the previous chunk, yet discarding the context breaks visual continuity, which limits approaches that edit the cache. Rather than editing the cache, we propose Residual Guidance (RG), a simple, training-free method that counteracts context inertia with a correction measured outside the cache. <details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>
