## 1. RoboTryOn: Synthesizing Dexterous Robot Demonstrations from Human Videos via Virtual Try-On / RoboTryOn ：通过虚拟试用从人类视频合成灵巧机器人演示

**Date**: 2026-11-03 | **arXiv**: [2611.05163v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05163v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.05163v1)

**Categories**: cs.RO, cs.AI

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Robot foundation models have shown remarkable performance in robot control, yet adapting them to a new task still relies heavily on teleoperated demonstrations. Egocentric human videos offer a scalable substitute, but overlaying rendered robots on human videos or retargeting human poses into robot actions leaves significant visual or action gaps for high-DoF robot embodiments. To address this limitation, we propose RoboTryOn, which treats human-to-robot translation as virtual try-on: try-on keeps the person and swaps the garment, RoboTryOn keeps the demonstrated motion and swaps the human for the robot. Specifically, RoboTryOn converts a human video into a video of the target robot specified by a reference image, while preserving the original task, objects, and background. To maintain visual consistency, we translate selected keyframes autoregressively and use them to guide video synthesis with motion conditioned on the human hand skeleton. Additionally, we use an inverse dynamics model (IDM) that incorporates depth features to infer action labels from synthesized videos for policy learning. Our experiments across multiple humanoid embodiments show that policies trained with our synthetic data improve the average success rate on novel tasks, including challenging bimanual manipulation, from 2% to 60%, without any real robot demonstration of those tasks. Moreover, training with our synthetic data matches the performance of policies trained solely on teleoperated demonstrations at approximately a third of the collection cost, demonstrating the cost efficiency of our approach.

## 2. Trajectory Evaluation via Rollout-Free World Model for End-to-end Autonomous Driving / 通过端到端自动驾驶的无推出世界模型进行轨迹评估

**Date**: 2026-11-03 | **arXiv**: [2611.05072v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05072v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.05072v1)

**Categories**: cs.RO, cs.AI, cs.CV

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Although existing candidate trajectory evaluators have substantially improved end-to-end planning accuracy, their underlying mechanism remains suboptimal. Model-based evaluators typically make scoring future-aware by explicitly predicting candidate-conditioned future states over the planning horizon, which we term rollout. However, rollout incurs high inference cost and accumulates prediction errors. We argue that planning does not require reconstructing a full future scene for each candidate, but only future-relevant information for reliable comparison. We therefore propose RFWorld, a Rollout-Free World model for end-to-end trajectory evaluation. RFWorld constructs a shared scene memory and shapes it into a future-readable representation via lightweight training-time temporal readout with semantic supervision, keeping training overhead low. At inference time, candidate trajectories directly query the shared memory via sparse spatial readout for scoring, reducing computation by roughly the planning horizon times the number of candidate trajectories, while avoiding recursive rollout errors. Experiments on NAVSIM and Bench2Drive show that RFWorld learns a more planning-aligned world representation and achieves state-of-the-art performance.

尽管现有的候选轨迹评估器大大提高了端到端规划的准确性，但其潜在的机制仍然不够理想。 基于模型的评估人员通常通过在规划范围内明确预测候选人条件的未来状态来进行评分，我们称之为推出。 但是，推出会产生高推理成本并累积预测误差。 我们认为，规划不需要为每个候选人重建完整的未来场景，而只需要与未来相关的信息，以便进行可靠的比较。 因此，我们提出了RFWorld ，这是一种用于端到端轨迹评估的Rollout-Free World模型。 RFWorld通过具有语义监督的轻量级训练时间时间读取构建共享场景内存并将其塑造成未来可读的表示，从而保持低训练开销。 在推理时，候选轨迹通过稀疏的空间读出直接查询共享内存进行评分，将计算量大致减少了规划地平线乘以候选轨迹的数量，同时避免了递归推出错误。 在NAVSIM和Bench2Drive上的实验表明， RFWorld学习了更符合规划的世界表示，并实现了最先进的性能。

## 3. Trusting Imagined Futures: Auditing Imagined Rewards for VLA Post-Training / 信任想象的未来：审核VLA培训后的想象奖励

**Date**: 2026-11-03 | **arXiv**: [2611.04873v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04873v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.04873v1)

**Categories**: cs.RO, cs.CV

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Video world models enable candidate actions to be evaluated through imagined outcomes, but high predicted rewards can favor trajectories with poor execution quality. We introduce Trusting Imagined Futures (TIF), an offline supervision selection method for vision-language-action (VLA) post-training that audits predicted rewards using execution feedback. A multidimensional reward model predicts a utility score for candidate ranking and five dimensions of execution quality. An auditor uses reward predictions and uncertainty to identify overestimation patterns learned from paired imagined videos and real executions. TIF accepts the candidate with the highest predicted utility when its advantage over the existing trajectory exceeds a margin and its audit risk passes a threshold; otherwise, it retains the existing supervision. The retained real trajectories then supervise behavior cloning. Across four LIBERO suites and three training seeds, TIF achieves a mean success rate of 76.25%, improving over training on the existing trajectories by 3.00 percentage points with the same trajectory count and training budget. Adding audit gating yields 1.47 percentage points with the reward model and advantage margin held fixed. The gains persist against advantage ranking and logistic regression risk selection at matched replacement counts. On recorded videos from three real robot tasks, TIF increases clean completion among selected trajectories from 58.33% to 70.00% across 60 candidate pools.

视频世界模型使候选人的行为能够通过想象的结果进行评估，但高预测奖励可能有利于执行质量差的轨迹。 我们引入了Trusting Imagined Futures （ TIF ） ，这是一种用于视觉语言行动（ VLA ）后期训练的离线监督选择方法，它使用执行反馈来审计预测的奖励。 多维奖励模型预测求职者排名的效用分数和执行质量的五个维度。 审核员使用奖励预测和不确定性来识别从配对的想象视频和实际执行中学到的高估模式。 当TIF在现有轨迹上的优势超过保证金并且其审计风险超过阈值时， TIF接受具有最高预测效用的候选人；否则，它保留现有的监督。 保留的真实轨迹然后监督行为克隆。 在四个LIBERO套件和三个培训种子中， TIF的平均成功率为76.25% ，在相同的轨迹计数和培训预算下，比现有轨迹的训练提高了3.00个百分点。 在奖励模型和优势保证金保持不变的情况下，添加审计门控收益率为1.47个百分点。 在匹配的替换计数下，优势排名和逻辑回归风险选择的收益持续存在。 在三个真实机器人任务的录制视频中， TIF将60个候选池中选定轨迹的干净完成率从58.33%提高到70.00%。

## 4. PrimeSim: A Generalist World Model Simulator from Large-Scale Cross-Embodiment Demonstrations / PrimeSim ：来自大规模交叉体演示的多面手世界模型模拟器

**Date**: 2026-11-03 | **arXiv**: [2611.04870v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04870v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.04870v1)

**Categories**: cs.RO

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

A robot world model that serves as a simulator must do more than generate plausible videos: it must faithfully follow commanded actions, maintain consistency across camera views, and sustain coherent long-horizon rollouts. We present PrimeSim, an action-conditioned, multiview world model simulator that addresses these challenges through progressive midtraining and posttraining. Starting from a pretrained video generator, we midtrain on 30,110 hours of robot demonstrations spanning 22 platforms. To improve action following, we introduce a unified cross-embodiment action representation and frame-aligned dual-path conditioning to strengthen action-frame correspondence. To maintain multiview consistency, we develop view-aware interleaved attention with explicit camera identities, enabling cross-view information exchange while preserving view-specific representations. We further introduce a fast-video, slow-memory posttraining framework that transforms the bidirectional video generator into an efficient autoregressive simulator. By combining fast causal generation, self-rollout training, and hierarchical temporal memory, PrimeSim mitigates error accumulation from self-generated histories and supports coherent long-horizon simulation. PrimeSim runs at 10.1 FPS, remains stable over long-horizon rollouts on rigid and deformable manipulation tasks, and transfers to held-out platforms within 100 to 200 gradient steps where the pretrained backbone does not. Against five open-source action-conditioned world models it leads four of the five WorldSimProbe suites and posts the best overall EWMBench score, while fine-grained local calibration still trails the field; its rollouts are faithful enough to support GRPO-based policy optimization. We release the model weights, inference code, and training code.

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

## 6. Future Representation Distillation for Imagination-Free World Action Models / 未来无想象力世界行动模型的代表性蒸馏

**Date**: 2026-11-03 | **arXiv**: [2611.04527v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04527v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.04527v1)

**Categories**: cs.RO

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

World Action Models (WAMs) use the predictive knowledge of pretrained video diffusion models for robot control through future video prediction. However, iterative video denoising is costly, and inaccurate visual predictions can compromise action prediction. Our key idea is to make this predictive knowledge available through visual representations computed from the current observation, enabling action prediction without generating future videos. We introduce DreamFree, a WAM that repurposes a pretrained video diffusion model as a predictive visual encoder through representation distillation. The visual encoder learns to predict a frozen video diffusion model's representation using only the current observation. An action module is then trained to predict actions by extracting action-relevant information from the visual encoder. Across real-world and simulated tasks, DreamFree achieves higher success rates than state-of-the-art VLAs and WAMs, while performing real-time closed-loop control at 11Hz with a 14B video diffusion backbone. Experiments show that DreamFree can translate stronger video priors and additional video data into better robot control and enable learning new robot tasks from video demonstrations, including videos from a different embodiment, without action annotation for the new tasks.

世界动作模型（ WAM ）使用预训练视频扩散模型的预测知识，通过未来的视频预测进行机器人控制。 然而，迭代视频去噪代价高昂，不准确的视觉预测可能会影响动作预测。 我们的关键思想是通过从当前观测中计算的视觉表示来提供这种预测知识，从而在不生成未来视频的情况下实现动作预测。 我们引入了DreamFree ，这是一种WAM ，通过表示提取将预训练的视频扩散模型重新用作预测性视觉编码器。 视觉编码器仅使用当前观测来学习预测冻结视频扩散模型的表示。 然后训练动作模块通过从视觉编码器中提取与动作相关的信息来预测动作。 在真实世界和模拟任务中， DreamFree的成功率高于最先进的VLA和WAM ，同时使用14B视频扩散骨干网在11Hz下执行实时闭环控制。 实验表明， DreamFree可以将更强的视频先验和额外的视频数据转化为更好的机器人控制，并能够从视频演示中学习新的机器人任务，包括来自不同实施例的视频，而无需对新任务进行操作注释。

## 7. Hybrid Particle-Voxel Dynamics for Multimodal 3D World Simulation / 用于多模态三维世界模拟的混合粒子-像素动力学

**Date**: 2026-11-03 | **arXiv**: [2611.04492v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04492v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.04492v1)

**Categories**: cs.RO, cs.CV, cs.LG

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Predicting how the world evolves in response to robot actions is central to policy learning, evaluation, and planning. However, many action-conditioned world models predict future observations in 2D image or video space without explicitly modeling the underlying 3D scene, making spatial consistency across viewpoints and over long-horizon interactions challenging. We introduce 3D World Simulator, an action-conditioned multimodal world modeling framework that jointly predicts appearance, geometry, and tactile observations from a unified 3D representation. Our approach combines a hybrid particle-voxel autoencoder that compresses point clouds into compact 3D voxel latents with an action-conditioned latent dynamics model that predicts their evolution. The shared 3D representation supports geometrically consistent rendering from arbitrary viewpoints and flexible integration of spatially aligned tactile signals. Our experiments demonstrate interactive, long-horizon, multimodal prediction at 50 Hz. We then show how our model enables scalable data generation for policy training and evaluation using tactile and multi-view visual observations, including wrist-mounted and novel camera views. Furthermore, we scale our method to large datasets containing diverse objects and demonstrate model-based planning with unseen objects and robot configurations. Together, these results highlight the potential of hybrid particle-voxel dynamics as a foundation for multimodal 3D world simulation.

预测世界如何响应机器人行动的演变是政策学习、评估和规划的核心。 然而，许多动作条件世界模型在没有明确建模底层3D场景的情况下预测2D图像或视频空间中的未来观测，使得跨视点和长视野交互的空间一致性具有挑战性。 我们推出了3D世界模拟器，这是一个动作条件多模态世界建模框架，可从统一的3D表示中联合预测外观、几何形状和触觉观察。 我们的方法将将点云压缩成紧凑型3D体素潜伏的混合粒子-体素自动编码器与预测其演变的动作条件潜伏动力学模型相结合。 共享3D表示支持从任意视点进行几何一致的渲染，并灵活集成空间对齐的触觉信号。 我们的实验演示了50 Hz下的交互式、长距离、多模态预测。 然后，我们展示了我们的模型如何使用触觉和多视图视觉观察（包括安装在手腕上的视图和新颖的相机视图）为策略培训和评估提供可扩展的数据生成。 此外，我们将我们的方法扩展到包含不同对象的大型数据集，并演示使用看不见的对象和机器人配置的基于模型的规划。 这些结果共同凸显了混合粒子-体素动力学作为多模态3D世界模拟基础的潜力。

## 8. Embodied AI for Scientific Discovery: From Physical Action to Scientific Evidence / 应用于科学发现的人工智能：从物理行为到科学证据

We organize the literature by three closures operating at distinct levels: operational closure (the experiment realized is the one intended), epistemic closure (the result updates beliefs or selects subsequent experiments) and evidential closure (the record supports a claim). We analyze workflows through a record-linked evidence cycle - Record, Diagnose, Calibrate, Confirm - as analytic functions with feedback. A taxonomy crosses these closures with six delegated decisions and five embodiment classes. Our formulation separates commanded, realized and observed conditions and models diagnosis with explicit resource, risk and sample-change costs. We compare experiment-selection methods by terminal decision, review perception, protocol compilation, visuomotor policies, world models, recovery and transfer by evidence emission requirements, and compare chemistry, materials, biology, microscopy and field workflows by what their records establish. An appendix provides an evidence map of 28 workflows (26 physical, two software-boundary), characterizing validation pathways and claim confirmation requirements. We propose evaluation that separates actions, valid experiments, campaigns and confirmed claims, framing open challenges as controlled comparisons with measurable failure conditions.

## 20. W2A: Distilling World Priors into Action Models for Real-Time Robotic Control / W2A ：将世界先验提炼成实时机器人控制的行动模型

**Categories**: cs.RO

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

World Action Models (WAMs) have recently shown great promise in robotic control, benefiting from the future imagination capability of large-scale pretrained video models. However, most existing WAMs require explicitly imagining future states at test time, incurring substantial computational overhead and latency that make them unsuitable for real-time robotic control. In this paper, we propose W2A, a two-stage distillation framework for real-time robotic manipulation that distills intermediate denoising representations from a video diffusion model into an action model during training, while entirely discarding the video model at test time. ## 21. Beyond Ensemble Disagreement: Predictive STL Monitoring of RL Locomotion under Distribution Shift / Beyond Ensemble Disagreement ：分布位移下RL运动的预测STL监测

**Categories**: cs.RO, cs.LG

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

RL locomotion policies trained under nominal simulation conditions may encounter distribution shifts that compromise safety. Predictive monitors could anticipate violations, but their world-model forecasts may become unreliable under shift, and ensemble disagreement is not calibrated.
