## 1. Sparse Planning in Visual World Models via Cost Gradients / 通过成本梯度在视觉世界模型中进行稀疏规划

**Date**: 2026-10-07 | **arXiv**: [2610.10274v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10274v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.10274v1)

**Categories**: cs.LG

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Token-based world models enable fine-grained latent planning, but repeatedly processing large spatial token grids makes action search expensive. We introduce COSTGRAD, a training-free, goal-conditioned selector that ranks spatial tokens by the gradient norm of the planning cost with respect to each input token. By deriving importance from the downstream control objective, COSTGRAD targets tokens that matter for planning rather than merely for prediction. On AdaLN-conditioned predictors at $50\%$ sparsity, COSTGRAD matches or exceeds full-token planning on three of four continuous-control benchmarks, while giving a measured $2.6\times$ wall-clock speedup per environment planning step. Combining token sparsity with reduced CEM search increases this to a $\sim 5\times$ total speedup while still exceeding the full-token baseline. We also identify an architecture-dependent failure mode: in a matched AdaLN-vs-concat comparison, concat maintains comparable full-token performance but pure COSTGRAD loses its advantage over random selection. This difference tracks action-pathway drift: gradient-selected removal produces less drift than random removal on AdaLN, but more on concat. These results highlight selector-architecture compatibility as a design axis for sparse world-model planning. Project page and demos: https://ycxuyingchen.github.io/costgrad/

基于令牌的世界模型可以实现细粒度的潜在规划，但重复处理大型空间令牌网格会使动作搜索变得昂贵。我们引入了 COSTGRAD，这是一种免训练、目标条件选择器，它根据每个输入标记的规划成本的梯度范数对空间标记进行排序。通过从下游控制目标中得出重要性，COSTGRAD 的目标是对规划而不是仅仅对预测重要的代币。在稀疏度为 50\%$ 的 AdaLN 条件预测器上，COSTGRAD 在四个连续控制基准中的三个上匹配或超过了全令牌规划，同时为每个环境规划步骤提供了测量到的 $2.6\times$ 挂钟加速。将令牌稀疏性与减少的 CEM 搜索相结合，可将总加速提高到 $\sim 5\times$，同时仍然超过全令牌基线。我们还确定了一种依赖于架构的故障模式：在匹配的 AdaLN 与 concat 比较中，concat 保持了可比较的全令牌性能，但纯 COSTGRAD 失去了相对于随机选择的优势。这种差异跟踪了动作路径漂移：在 AdaLN 上，梯度选择删除产生的漂移比随机删除少，但在串联上产生的漂移更多。这些结果强调了选择器架构兼容性作为稀疏世界模型规划的设计轴。项目页面和演示：https://ycxuyingchen.github.io/costgrad/

## 2. Identifiability of a dissipative knowledge-dynamics model: exact recovery under designed excitation, degeneration on observational data / 耗散知识动力学模型的可识别性：设计激励下的精确恢复、观测数据的退化

**Date**: 2026-10-07 | **arXiv**: [2610.09889v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.09889v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.09889v1)

**Categories**: cs.LG, math.OC, stat.ML

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Human learning is a dissipative dynamical process: mastery accumulates through practice, decays through forgetting, and propagates across interdependent concepts. We model it as a nonlinear dissipative system of ordinary differential equations whose parameters are mechanistically meaningful (a concept-transfer matrix encoding prerequisite coupling, per-concept forgetting rates, and a saturating practice-response gain), and we study when those parameters can actually be recovered from data. We prove a structural identifiability theorem for the associated inverse problem under explicit excitation conditions, with constructive closed-form recovery for the two-concept case, together with monotonicity, robustness and L-stability results. We derive a semi-implicit L-stable scheme for the dissipative subsystem and a batched solver numerically equivalent to the per-trajectory formulation (bit-exact predictions, gradients to $10^{-10}$) yet two orders of magnitude faster, making estimation feasible on cohorts of $10^5$ learners. The empirical study is two-sided. Under the theorem's excitation conditions, synthetic recovery is exact: parameters to machine precision, prerequisite structure at $F_1 = 1.0$. On large observational benchmarks it is not. An apparently strong recovery, with forgetting rates correlating with topic difficulty at Spearman $ρ= 0.83$, is refuted by four independent controls: it survives destroying the temporal order of the data, is matched by a classical Bayesian baseline, and is unaffected by removing real timestamps. We trace this to the stationary structure of the model and show that it is the degeneration the theorem predicts in the absence of designed excitation. The result delineates a sharp boundary between identifiable and unidentifiable regimes and yields a validation protocol for interpretability claims.

人类学习是一个耗散的动态过程：掌握通过实践积累，通过遗忘衰减，并在相互依赖的概念中传播。我们将其建模为常微分方程的非线性耗散系统，其参数具有机械意义（编码先决条件耦合的概念转移矩阵、每个概念的遗忘率和饱和练习响应增益），并且我们研究何时可以真正从数据中恢复这些参数。我们证明了显式激励条件下相关反问题的结构可辨识定理，以及两个概念情况的建设性闭式恢复，以及单调性、鲁棒性和 L 稳定性结果。我们为耗散子系统推导了一个半隐式 L 稳定方案，并在数值上相当于每个轨迹公式的批处理求解器（位精确预测，梯度为 10^{-10}$），但速度快了两个数量级，使得对 10^5$ 学习者群体的估计变得可行。实证研究是双面的。在定理的激励条件下，综合恢复是精确的：参数达到机器精度，先决条件结构为 $F_1 = 1.0$。在大型观察基准上并非如此。明显强劲的恢复，遗忘率与 Spearman $ρ= 0.83$ 的主题难度相关，被四个独立的控制所驳斥：它在破坏数据的时间顺序的情况下幸存下来，与经典贝叶斯基线匹配，并且不受删除真实时间戳的影响。我们将其追溯到模型的稳态结构，并表明该定理预测的是在没有设计激励的情况下的退化。结果描绘了可识别和不可识别制度之间的清晰界限，并产生了可解释性声明的验证协议。

</details>

## 3. Beyond Policy Support: Interaction Constrained Offline Reinforcement Learning for Autonomous Driving / 政策支持之外：交互约束的离线强化学习自动驾驶

**Date**: 2026-10-07 | **arXiv**: [2610.09763v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.09763v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.09763v1)

**Categories**: cs.LG, cs.AI, cs.RO

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Offline reinforcement learning enables reward-driven policy improvement from fixed datasets without requiring online exploration, making it particularly attractive in safety-critical domains. A central challenge, however, is distribution shift: policy optimization may favor actions that are weakly supported by the offline data, rendering value estimates unreliable. Existing approaches primarily control this shift in the policy's own action space. In interactive environments such as autonomous driving, this can be insufficient: a candidate ego trajectory may remain well supported under the marginal behavior distribution while being poorly supported jointly with the surrounding-agent behavior observed in the logged interaction. We refer to this degradation in interaction support as \emph{interaction distribution shift} (IDS), and introduce \emph{Interaction-Constrained Drive Policy} (ICDP), an offline reinforcement learning framework that explicitly controls interaction-level distribution shift. Starting from the joint data distribution over ego and surrounding-agent futures, we show that joint-support degradation decomposes exactly into an ego-support component and a residual interaction-support component. We recover the latter through contrastive density-ratio estimation, isolating interaction compatibility without explicit joint-density modeling, surrounding-agent prediction, or rollouts in reactive simulators or learned world models during policy optimization. Closed-loop evaluations on nuPlan, Interplan and real-world truck experiments show that ICDP suppresses high-value yet interaction-unsupported trajectory selections and improves performance in interaction-critical driving scenarios. Project webpage: https://mahmoud-selim.github.io/ICDP/

离线强化学习可以从固定数据集进行奖励驱动的策略改进，而无需在线探索，这使其在安全关键领域特别有吸引力。然而，一个核心挑战是分配转移：政策优化可能有利于离线数据支持较弱的行动，从而导致价值估计不可靠。现有方法主要控制政策自身行动空间的这种转变。在自动驾驶等交互式环境中，这可能是不够的：候选自我轨迹可能在边缘行为分布下保持良好的支持，而与记录的交互中观察到的周围智能体行为共同得到较差的支持。我们将交互支持的这种退化称为\emph{交互分布偏移}（IDS），并引入\emph{交互约束驱动策略}（ICDP），这是一种离线强化学习框架，可以显式控制交互级别的分布偏移。从自我和周围智能体未来的联合数据分布开始，我们表明联合支持退化完全分解为自我支持组件和剩余交互支持组件。我们通过对比密度比估计来恢复后者，无需显式联合密度建模、周围代理预测或在策略优化期间在反应模拟器或学习世界模型中推出，即可隔离交互兼容性。对 nuPlan、Interplan 和现实世界卡车实验的闭环评估表明，ICDP 抑制了高价值但不支持交互的轨迹选择，并提高了交互关键驾驶场景中的性能。项目网页：https://mahmoud-selim.github.io/ICDP/

</details>
