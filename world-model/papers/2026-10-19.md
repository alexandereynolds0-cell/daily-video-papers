## 2. TransNav: Transformer-Based End-to-End Navigation Method for Static and Dynamic Quadrotor Obstacle Avoidance / TransNav ：静态和动态四旋翼避障的基于变压器的端到端导航方法

**Date**: 2026-10-16 | **arXiv**: [2610.23635v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23635v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.23635v1)

**Categories**: cs.RO

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Obstacle avoidance for autonomous quadrotors in cluttered environments remains challenging, especially when both dense static obstacles and moving obstacles are present. Existing methods are often tailored to either static or dynamic settings, which always limit their adaptability across diverse environments. This paper presents TransNav, a transformer-based end-to-end navigation method for quadrotor obstacle avoidance in both static and dynamic environments. TransNav employs a patch-based point-cloud encoding scheme that combines local feature extraction with global contextual modeling, enabling compact yet informative environment representation. The method integrates perception and navigation in a single architecture and directly predicts a finite-horizon action sequence, from which the first action is executed. Moreover, we develop an unsupervised learning strategy by incorporating a lightweight point-mass dynamics model, which directly optimizes trajectory-level costs over the predicted sequence through numerical gradient propagation. Thus, the policy can be efficiently trained without expert demonstrations. Simulation and real-world experiments show that the proposed method achieves strong performance in dense static environments and competitive obstacle-avoidance capability in dynamic scenarios, while maintaining low inference cost for onboard deployment.

在杂乱的环境中，自主四旋翼的避障仍然具有挑战性，特别是当存在密集的静态障碍物和移动障碍物时。 现有方法通常针对静态或动态设置进行定制，这始终限制了它们在不同环境中的适应性。 本文提出了TransNav ，一种基于变压器的端到端导航方法，用于在静态和动态环境中避开四旋翼障碍物。 TransNav采用基于补丁的点云编码方案，将局部特征提取与全局上下文建模相结合，实现紧凑而信息丰富的环境表示。 该方法将感知和导航集成在单个架构中，并直接预测执行第一个动作的有限视野动作序列。 此外，我们通过结合轻量级点质量动力学模型开发了一种无监督学习策略，该模型通过数值梯度传播直接优化预测序列的轨迹级成本。 因此，该政策可以在没有专家演示的情况下进行有效的培训。 仿真和实际实验表明，该方法在密集的静态环境中具有较强的性能，在动态场景中具有较强的避障能力，同时保持了较低的机载部署推理成本。

## 3. Beyond Action Prediction: Learning Search-Free Planning in Latent Space / 超越行动预测：在潜在空间中学习无需搜索的规划

**Date**: 2026-10-16 | **arXiv**: [2610.23615v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23615v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.23615v1)

**Categories**: cs.RO, cs.AI

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Latent world models learn action-conditioned state transitions in a compact representation space, providing a predictive model for visual planning and control. However, planning with these models typically requires costly test-time optimization over action sequences. We propose to replace this online search with a goal-conditioned inverse model that directly predicts actions from current and goal observations. A key challenge is that action prediction alone neither ensures that goal representations encode action-relevant information nor that predicted actions are consistent with the learned latent prediction model. We introduce Hindsight Inverse JEPA Planning (HIJP), a search-free framework that addresses both aspects during training. HIJP relabels future observations along offline trajectories as goals, providing action supervision across multiple temporal horizons. By retaining gradients through the goal representations, this supervision directly shapes the visual representation. HIJP further feeds predicted actions into the latent predictor model and aligns the resulting next-state representations with encoded successor observations, enforcing action-transition consistency. At inference time, the inverse dynamics model and latent predictor models are applied alternately to generate multi-step action sequences without online search. Across four visual control tasks, HIJP achieves 86.8% overall success and a 1.9-16.5x planning speedup over search-based planning.

潜在世界模型在紧凑的表示空间中学习动作条件状态转换，为视觉规划和控制提供预测模型。 然而，使用这些模型进行规划通常需要对动作序列进行昂贵的测试时间优化。 我们建议将此在线搜索替换为目标条件逆模型，该模型可直接从当前和目标观察中预测行动。 一个关键的挑战是，动作预测本身既不能确保目标表示编码与动作相关的信息，也不能确保预测的动作与学习的潜在预测模型一致。 我们引入了后见之明反向JEPA规划（ HIJP ） ，这是一个在培训期间解决两方面问题的无搜索框架。 HIJP将未来的观察结果沿着离线轨迹重新标记为目标，提供跨多个时间视野的行动监督。 通过目标表示保留梯度，这种监督直接塑造了视觉表示。 HIJP进一步将预测的动作馈送到潜在预测模型中，并将生成的下一状态表示与编码的后继观测值对齐，从而增强动作转换的一致性。 在推理时，逆动力学模型和潜预测器模型交替应用，无需在线搜索即可生成多步动作序列。 在四项视觉控制任务中， HIJP取得了86.8%的整体成功，比基于搜索的计划加快了1.9-16.5倍的规划速度。

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

In many real-world scenarios we need to consider formal requirements, such as safety and constraint compliance, to deploy Reinforcement Learning (RL) agents. In this paper we introduce RecReg, a safe RL framework capable of enforcing non-Markovian regular safety properties via recovery-based shielding. RecReg is a unified approach that supports different levels of a priori knowledge and safety guarantees. The paper primarily focuses on building the theoretical foundations for RecReg in the tabular setting, providing rigorous safety and convergence guarantees that are not present in related work. We provide an extensive set of empirical results using the MASA-Safe-RL library, and we outline an extension of RecReg to deep RL domains using SOTA model-based RL. Our empirical results demonstrate that RecReg achieves efficient learning and matches the theory in this paper.

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Rapid progress in embodied intelligence has enabled increasingly capable robots to follow language instructions and manipulate diverse objects. To address this perceptual gap, a virtual active-perception paradigm is introduced.
