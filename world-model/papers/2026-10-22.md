## 1. Agentic World Action Models / 代理世界行动模型

**Date**: 2026-10-21 | **arXiv**: [2610.30136v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30136v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.30136v1)

**Categories**: cs.RO

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Recent world action models provide visual foresight and future prediction for robot manipulation, but lack explicit mechanisms for compositional skill reuse. Agentic robotics combines code-based planning and reasoning with reusable skills, but often lacks predictive grounding in physical dynamics. We introduce AW-0, an agentic world action model framework that integrates code-based planning with joint video and action prediction. We jointly model code, future video, and robot actions as three coupled generative streams within a unified mixture-of-transformers architecture. Within this architecture a code expert generates parameterized skill routines and control logic while video and action experts generate future visual states and low-level motor commands. Code serves two complementary roles: it structures multi-step prediction and control within the world model and represents skills as reusable programs for subsequent agentic execution. We further introduce an automated curation pipeline that labels robot and human video trajectories with aligned programs and consolidates recurring routines into reusable skill libraries. Evaluations on RoboTwin 2.0 and physical bimanual manipulation tasks show improved task performance over VLA and WAM baselines, alongside gains in language following, semantic reasoning and robustness, demonstrating that agentic code enables connection between planning, prediction and execution within WAMs.

最近的世界动作模型为机器人操作提供了视觉远见和未来预测，但缺乏用于组合技能重用的明确机制。代理机器人技术将基于代码的规划和推理与可重用的技能结合起来，但通常缺乏物理动力学的预测基础。我们介绍 AW-0，一种代理世界动作模型框架，它将基于代码的规划与联合视频和动作预测相集成。我们将代码、未来视频和机器人动作联合建模为统一的混合变压器架构中的三个耦合生成流。在此架构中，代码专家生成参数化的技能例程和控制逻辑，而视频和动作专家生成未来的视觉状态和低级运动命令。代码具有两个互补的作用：它在世界模型中构建多步骤预测和控制，并将技能表示为可重用程序以供后续代理执行。我们进一步引入了一个自动管理管道，该管道使用对齐的程序来标记机器人和人类视频轨迹，并将重复的例程整合到可重用的技能库中。对 RoboTwin 2.0 和物理双手操作任务的评估显示，任务性能比 VLA 和 WAM 基线有所提高，同时在语言跟踪、语义推理和鲁棒性方面也有所提高，这表明代理代码可以在 WAM 内实现规划、预测和执行之间的连接。

</details>

## 2. PhysiCalWorld: In-Context Physics Calibration for World Models / PhysiCalWorld：世界模型的上下文物理校准

**Date**: 2026-10-21 | **arXiv**: [2610.30125v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30125v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.30125v1)

**Categories**: cs.RO, cs.AI, cs.CV

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Existing world models typically perceive a scene through current visual frame or a narrow history window. However, these observations may not reveal the physics properties that determine transition dynamics, such as an object's mass distribution, joint damping, or surface friction. Without calibrating these physics factors for world models, predictions tend to regress toward an empirical prior and induce severe drift in novel test scenes. To this end, we propose PhysiCalWorld, which calibrates action-conditioned world models to the instance-specific dynamics via in-context prompting, without fine-tuning or analytical physics modeling. Our method encodes an exploratory interaction clip collected within the test scene into a compact physics descriptor token, which grounds subsequent predictions in the scene's actual physical behavior. PhysiCalWorld is trained end-to-end with the latent prediction loss alone, without physics labels. At inference time, the descriptor token is extracted once and can be reused across rollouts with negligible compute overhead. We evaluate PhysiCalWorld across four environments covering diverse physics variations and show that it substantially improves rollout prediction accuracy and visual planning success rate. The learned descriptors also form a physically structured latent space and generalize beyond the physics ranges seen during training.

现有的世界模型通常通过当前的视觉框架或狭窄的历史窗口来感知场景。然而，这些观察结果可能无法揭示决定过渡动力学的物理特性，例如物体的质量分布、关节阻尼或表面摩擦。如果不针对世界模型校准这些物理因素，预测往往会回归到经验先验，并在新的测试场景中引起严重的漂移。为此，我们提出了 PhysiCalWorld，它通过上下文提示将动作条件世界模型校准到特定于实例的动态，而无需微调或分析物理建模。我们的方法将测试场景中收集的探索性交互片段编码为紧凑的物理描述符标记，这为后续预测场景的实际物理行为奠定了基础。 PhysiCalWorld 仅使用潜在预测损失进行端到端训练，没有物理标签。在推理时，描述符令牌被提取一次，并且可以在各个部署中重复使用，而计算开销可以忽略不计。我们在涵盖不同物理变化的四种环境中评估 PhysiCalWorld，并表明它大大提高了推出预测的准确性和视觉规划的成功率。学习到的描述符还形成了物理结构的潜在空间，并泛化到训练期间看到的物理范围之外。

</details>

## 3. Events, Not Pixels: Where World Models Get the Return Wrong / 事件，而不是像素：世界模型返回错误的地方

**Date**: 2026-10-21 | **arXiv**: [2610.29435v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.29435v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.29435v1)

**Categories**: cs.AI

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

World models are increasingly used for planning, where a planner ranks candidate actions by returns imagined by the model. However, these models are judged mainly by how well they render frames, even though a visually faithful rollout can still assign the wrong return. To identify where this mismatch arises, we introduce a transition-level diagnostic that replaces imagined transitions with real ones and measures the reduction in return error against a budget-matched random control. Our analysis yields two findings. (1) Event transitions, the few steps where a reward is earned or an episode ends, are rendered as well as other steps (median pixel-error ratio 0.85), yet restoring them removes a median of 70% of the return error. In contrast, restoring transitions with the largest MSE, SSIM or LPIPS error removes less than a random choice. Across the 44 Atari-100k model--collection pairs that pass our reliability checks, including three released families, event patches outperform random patches in 39 (sign test p=1.4 x 10^{-7}), with a median gain of +44 percentage points. (2) The remaining error is structured: a complementary patch separates errors at true events from those elsewhere, while an eight-step timing tolerance recovers few events missed by our deterministic models. This structure also guides repair: a rule recorded before four repair outcomes were known predicts when a diffusion model repairs these failures. Event patches improve plan ranking, and the same concentration appears on control tasks with rare reward events.

世界模型越来越多地用于规划，规划者根据模型想象的回报对候选行动进行排名。然而，这些模型主要是根据它们渲染帧的效果来判断的，即使视觉上忠实的推出仍然可以分配错误的回报。为了确定这种不匹配出现的位置，我们引入了一种转换级诊断，用真实的转换替换想象的转换，并测量相对于预算匹配的随机控制的返回误差的减少量。我们的分析得出两个结果。 (1) 事件转换（获得奖励或剧集结束的几个步骤）以及其他步骤（中位像素错误率 0.85）都会被渲染，但恢复它们会消除 70% 的返回错误的中位值。相比之下，用最大的 MSE、SSIM 或 LPIPS 误差恢复转换所消除的随机选择要少。在通过我们可靠性检查的 44 个 Atari-100k 模型集合对中，包括三个已发布的系列，事件补丁在 39 个中优于随机补丁（符号检验 p=1.4 x 10^{-7}），中值增益 +44 个百分点。 (2) 剩余的错误是结构化的：补充补丁将真实事件的错误与其他地方的错误分开，而八步时序容差则恢复了我们的确定性模型遗漏的少数事件。这种结构还指导修复：在已知四个修复结果之前记录的规则可以预测扩散模型何时修复这些故障。事件补丁提高了计划排名，并且同样的集中度出现在具有稀有奖励事件的控制任务上。

</details>

## 4. LLM-Based Exogenous World Modeling for Dynamic Scheduling / 基于法学硕士的动态调度外生世界建模

**Date**: 2026-10-21 | **arXiv**: [2610.29308v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.29308v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.29308v1)

**Categories**: cs.AI, cs.CL

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Dynamic scheduling requires dispatching online arrivals while anticipating future jobs, as poor decisions can compound downstream costs under nonlinear tardiness objectives. We show that a pretrained large language model (LLM) can serve as the exogenous component of a structured world model, forecasting uncertain future job arrivals. Specifically, we exploit an afterstate decomposition, which separates known deterministic shop transitions from uncertain exogenous job arrivals, enabling the LLM to forecast only the latter through a compact belief representation. Building on this, we develop Afterstate World-Model Planning (AWMP), which combines an evolved, belief-conditioned dispatching strategy with short scenario-based rollouts. A surprise-triggered refresh mechanism queries the LLM only when newly observed arrivals deviate sufficiently from previous forecast, and online residual self-calibration further conditions the LLM on its recent forecast errors to handle nonstationary arrivals. Across diverse arrival distributions, AWMP reduces weighted tardiness by 6-21% on dynamic job-shop and 22-46% on flexible job-shop benchmarks, relative to the best of eight classical priority-dispatching rules.

动态调度需要在预测未来工作的同时调度在线到达的人员，因为糟糕的决策可能会在非线性迟到目标下增加下游成本。我们证明，预训练的大语言模型（LLM）可以作为结构化世界模型的外生组件，预测不确定的未来工作到来。具体来说，我们利用事后分解，将已知的确定性车间转换与不确定的外生工作到达分开，使法学硕士能够通过紧凑的信念表示仅预测后者。在此基础上，我们开发了 Afterstate 世界模型规划 (AWMP)，它将不断发展的、基于信念的调度策略与基于场景的短期部署相结合。仅当新观察到的到达事件与之前的预测有足够偏差时，意外触发的刷新机制才会查询 LLM，并且在线残差自校准进一步调节 LLM 的最近预测误差，以处理非平稳到达事件。在不同的到达分布中，相对于八种经典优先调度规则中的最佳规则，AWMP 在动态作业车间中将加权迟到率降低了 6-21%，在灵活作业车间基准上将加权迟到率降低了 22-46%。

</details>

## 5. How Representational Priors Shape Generalization in World Action Models / 代表性先验如何塑造世界行动模型的泛化

**Date**: 2026-10-21 | **arXiv**: [2610.29168v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.29168v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.29168v1)

**Categories**: cs.RO, cs.AI, cs.CV

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

World action models (WAMs) are shown to substantially diverge under generalization performance based on their generative backbones. We investigate this gap through the representational geometry that WAMs inherit from their backbones and how policy training reorganizes it. Our central hypothesis is that a useful representational prior would structure geometry that separates nuisance information from the in-distribution manifold in advance of training as a policy. We quantify this with nuisance orthogonality; orthogonality of the feature under visual variation outside the empirical in-distribution manifold. Across various image and video backbones, we show that a higher orthogonality from the representational prior is associated with stronger robustness to visual variations after WAM training, enabling policy generalization measurable prospectively for world models. In addition, analyzing the resulting robust WAMs reveals nuisance suppression; relative suppression of feature displacement caused by such variations and robust action generation of the policy. Together, these findings provide a geometric perspective on how the representational prior of the world model shapes the generalization performance of WAMs.

世界行动模型（WAM）根据其生成主干的泛化性能表现出很大的差异。我们通过 WAM 从其主干继承的表征几何结构以及政策训练如何重新组织它来研究这一差距。我们的中心假设是，有用的表征先验将构造几何结构，在作为策略进行训练之前将有害信息与分布流形分开。我们用令人讨厌的正交性来量化这一点；经验分布流形之外的视觉变化下特征的正交性。在各种图像和视频主干中，我们表明，与表征先验的更高正交性与 WAM 训练后对视觉变化的更强鲁棒性相关，从而使世界模型的策略泛化能够进行前瞻性测量。此外，分析所得的稳健 WAM 揭示了滋扰抑制；由这种变化和策略的稳健动作生成引起的特征位移的相对抑制。总之，这些发现为世界模型的表征先验如何塑造 WAM 的泛化性能提供了几何视角。

</details>

## 6. Steering Black-Box Large Language Model Agents with Pitfall Anticipation World Model / 用陷阱预期世界模型引导黑盒大型语言模型智能体

**Date**: 2026-10-21 | **arXiv**: [2610.29139v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.29139v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.29139v1)

**Categories**: cs.AI

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Frontier black-box Large Language Model (LLM) agents are proficient at long-horizon reasoning and precise action generation. However, when it comes to a specific agentic task, they have no internalized knowledge of how that particular environment unfolds. A world model offers a natural remedy, providing the agent with environment specific knowledge by foreseeing how the environment will respond to the action it is about to take. Prior world models for LLM agents mainly predict next observations at each step, which is costly and often uninformative because individual observations provide limited task-relevant guidance and not every step requires call for foresight. This raises a key question: what a world model should predict and when should it be invoked for agentic tasks? Our pilot study suggests that world model guidance is most useful when it anticipates pitfalls within a bounded multi-step future. Motivated by this, we introduce Pitfall Anticipation World Model (PAWM), which selectively intervenes when a potential pitfall arises and predicts the impending failure to steer the agent away from it. PAWM is trained in two stages: Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) on synthetic data labeled with agent pitfalls, followed by GRPO co-training with a black-box LLM to further optimize it in the target environment. PAWM improves Claude Sonnet 4.5 performance by 24.3% on ScienceWorld and 7.7% on WebArena-Lite, outperforming a range of baselines. These results demonstrate that PAWM provides an effective form of world model guidance for black-box LLM agents.

Frontier 黑盒大型语言模型 (LLM) 代理精通长视野推理和精确动作生成。然而，当涉及到特定的代理任务时，他们对特定环境如何展开并没有内在的了解。世界模型提供了一种自然的补救措施，通过预见环境将如何响应它将要采取的行动，为智能体提供特定于环境的知识。 LLM 智能体的先前世界模型主要预测每一步的下一步观察，这是昂贵的且通常缺乏信息，因为个体观察提供的与任务相关的指导有限，并且并非每一步都需要远见。这就提出了一个关键问题：世界模型应该预测什么以及何时应该调用它来执行代理任务？我们的试点研究表明，当世界模型指导预测有限的多步骤未来内的陷阱时，它是最有用的。受此启发，我们引入了陷阱预期世界模型（PAWM），该模型在潜在陷阱出现时选择性地进行干预，并预测即将发生的故障，从而引导智能体远离它。 PAWM 分两个阶段进行训练：对标记有代理陷阱的合成数据进行监督微调 (SFT)，然后与黑盒 LLM 进行 GRPO 联合训练，以在目标环境中进一步优化它。 PAWM 将 Claude Sonnet 4.5 在 ScienceWorld 上的性能提高了 24.3%，在 WebArena-Lite 上提高了 7.7%，优于一系列基线。这些结果表明，PAWM 为黑盒 LLM 代理提供了一种有效的世界模型指导形式。

</details>

## 7. Causal world models under physical parameter shifts / 物理参数变化下的因果世界模型

**Date**: 2026-10-21 | **arXiv**: [2610.29088v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.29088v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.29088v1)

**Categories**: cs.LG, cs.RO

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

World models predict how embodied systems evolve under actions, but their accuracy can degrade when physical conditions move outside the training distribution. We study two components of causal modeling: which inputs a predictor may use, and how its mechanism computes their effects. In a synthetic benchmark with shortcut features, a frozen dependency mask derived from paired interventions reduces mean out-of-distribution (OOD) RMSE from 1.769 for a fully connected predictor to 0.697, close to the generator-oracle mask (0.694). For robot link-mass extrapolation in CausalWorld, with disjoint training and test mass ranges, we develop finger-local world models in which link masses enter inertia and dynamics-bias terms through explicit affine coefficients, actions act through either a learned torque network or the known PD controller, and learned residuals capture contact-related effects. Compared with a separately trained control whose mass inputs are fixed at training means, the known-PD model turns out-of-range masses into a consistent predictive gain: median time-averaged joint R2 over 150-step rollouts rises from 0.609 to 0.727, with improvement in all ten task-seed runs (exact two-sided sign test, p = 0.002). In a shared 100-step forecast, the learned-torque model is competitive with the strongest recurrent robotic world model (RWM) configuration (median joint R2 of 0.755 - 0.768 versus 0.752), whereas supplying out-of-range masses sharply degrades the MLP and recurrent predictors. These results indicate that how a physical parameter enters the dynamics mechanism determines whether its out-of-range value helps or harms prediction.

世界模型预测具体系统在行动下如何演变，但当物理条件超出训练分布时，其准确性可能会降低。我们研究因果建模的两个组成部分：预测器可能使用哪些输入，以及其机制如何计算其影响。在具有快捷功能的综合基准中，源自配对干预的冻结依赖性掩码将完全连接预测器的平均分布外 (OOD) RMSE 从 1.769 降低到 0.697，接近生成器-预言机掩码 (0.694)。对于 CausalWorld 中的机器人链接质量外推，通过不相交的训练和测试质量范围，我们开发了手指局部世界模型，其中链接质量通过显式仿射系数输入惯性和动力学偏差项，动作通过学习的扭矩网络或已知的 PD 控制器起作用，学习的残差捕获接触相关的效应。与质量输入固定在训练平均值的单独训练的控制相比，已知的 PD 模型将超出范围的质量转化为一致的预测增益：150 步推出的中值时间平均联合 R2 从 0.609 上升到 0.727，所有 10 次任务种子运行都有所改善（精确的双边符号检验，p = 0.002）。在共享的 100 步预测中，学习扭矩模型与最强的循环机器人世界模型 (RWM) 配置具有竞争力（中值联合 R2 为 0.755 - 0.768 与 0.752），而提供超出范围的质量会急剧降低 MLP 和循环预测器的性能。这些结果表明，物理参数如何进入动力学机制决定了其超出范围的值是否有助于或损害预测。

</details>

## 8. OccJEPA: Occlusion as Masking for Amodal Video Segmentation / OccJEPA：遮挡作为阿模态视频分割的掩蔽

**Date**: 2026-10-21 | **arXiv**: [2610.29061v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.29061v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.29061v1)

**Categories**: cs.CV, cs.LG

**Code**: https://github.com/zgp110/occjepa.

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Amodal video segmentation asks for the complete mask of an object in every frame, including the parts hidden behind other objects. The best current methods generate these masks with fine-tuned video diffusion models, which need many denoising steps and an extra depth network, and give a different answer for every sample. Self-supervised video world models of the JEPA family are trained on a closely related task: predicting the representation of masked space-time regions from the visible ones. We therefore treat occlusion as masking. The tokens that the object's visible mask does not cover are dropped, the pre-trained V-JEPA 2.1 predictor fills them in, and a light head decodes the observed and predicted features into amodal masks. The resulting model, OccJEPA (Occlusion-as-masking JEPA), is deterministic and 33.5x faster than the diffusion-based state of the art, and more accurate on SAIL-VOS, on TAO-Amodal, including a held-out test split (+4.00 AP50) and visible masks tracked by SAM2, and on Kubric. Controlled experiments trace the gain to the predictor's pre-trained weights rather than to its capacity, for world models from 87M to 1.85B parameters. Code is available at https://github.com/zgp110/occjepa.

阿模态视频分割要求每个帧中对象的完整掩模，包括隐藏在其他对象后面的部分。当前最好的方法使用微调的视频扩散模型生成这些掩模，这需要许多去噪步骤和额外的深度网络，并为每个样本给出不同的答案。 JEPA 系列的自监督视频世界模型接受了一项密切相关的任务的训练：从可见区域中预测遮蔽时空区域的表示。因此，我们将遮挡视为掩蔽。对象的可见掩模未覆盖的标记被丢弃，预训练的 V-JEPA 2.1 预测器填充它们，并且光头将观察到的和预测的特征解码为非模态掩模。生成的模型 OccJEPA（遮挡作为掩蔽 JEPA）是确定性的，比基于扩散的现有技术快 33.5 倍，并且在 SAIL-VOS、TAO-Amodal 上更准确，包括保留的测试分割 (+4.00 AP50) 和 SAM2 和 Kubric 跟踪的可见掩模。对于 87M 到 1.85B 参数的世界模型，对照实验将增益追踪到预测器的预训练权重，而不是其容量。代码可在 https://github.com/zgp110/occjepa 获取。

</details>

## 9. Self-Improving Agents with World Model Predictive Paths / 具有世界模型预测路径的自我改进代理

**Date**: 2026-10-20 | **arXiv**: [2610.28945v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28945v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.28945v1)

**Categories**: cs.AI

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Large language model (LLM) agents are increasingly expected to interact with environments and improve their decisions during deployment. However, how anticipating future outcomes and drawing on accumulated interaction experience can jointly support decision-making remains underexplored. We propose a test-time framework, inspired by Model Predictive Path Integral (MPPI) control, that combines multi-branch predictive analysis with experience-based adaptation. At each step, the agent proposes multiple candidate actions and imagines their outcomes with a world model, so that alternatives can be compared without being executed in the environment. 视频生成模型是体现世界模型的有希望的基础，但前提是它们生成的展示在物理上有效并且忠实于任务指令。多模态大语言模型（MLLM）越来越多地用于判断这些属性，但它们与人类对具体视频的判断的一致性尚未得到系统评估。为了解决这一差距，我们引入了 RoboJudge-Bench，这是一个评估 MLLM 作为具体视频生成法官的基准。该基准衡量两个基本维度：物理依从性和指令对齐，涵盖来自 8 个源语料库和 10 个视频生成器的 800 个人工注释视频，涵盖抓手、人手和灵巧手。实验揭示了现有法官和人类评分之间的不一致：通用 MLLM 在物理依从性方面表现出较低的一致性，而为通用领域物理推理设计的专门视频法官并不能始终如一地转移到具体场景。受这些发现的启发，我们构建了 RoboJudge-Train，其中包含 12K 视频，注释有 25K 人类验证的分数以及物理依从性和指令对齐的模型辅助原理。我们通过监督学习和强化学习在此数据集上微调 9B MLLM，产生 RoboJudge-9B。在 RoboJudge-Bench 上评估的模型法官中，RoboJudge-9B 实现了最高的总体一致性，达到 0.719 的皮尔逊相关性，与我们评估中表现最佳的专有基线 Gemini-3.7-Flash 相比，具有统计显着性的 0.089 增益。 RoboJudge-Train 上的训练还可以在外部基准测试（包括 VideoPhy2 和 Physion-Eval）上产生收益。我们进一步使用 RoboJudge-9B 作为下游评估器，根据物理粘附和指令对齐来对视频生成模型进行基准测试。

## 17. Training Controllable World Models via Action Influence Maximization / 通过行动影响力最大化训练可控世界模型

**Date**: 2026-10-19 | **arXiv**: [2610.27387v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27387v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.27387v1)

**Categories**: cs.RO, cs.LG

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Action-conditioned video generative models trained on successful, goal-directed demonstrations tend to ignore the commanded action, rendering a plausible expert-like future regardless of what is asked of them. We trace this failure to training data that lack sufficient action influence. We analyze this metric and prove that it upper bounds the action influence that any model trained on that data can express. Expert demonstrations, whose narrow action distributions let next states be predicted without attending to the action, set a low ceiling that no amount of model capacity alone can raise. Raising it requires collecting different data. We introduce Action Influence Maximization (AIM), an algorithm for actively collecting training data for improving dataset action influence. We compare the performance and data-efficiency of AIM against exploration heuristics and intrinsic-motivation methods on simulated navigation and manipulation tasks. In pixel-based manipulation, AIM is the only method that consistently improves controllability: 500 actively collected episodes added to 3k demonstrations match a world model trained on 300k expert episodes on end-effector controllability, an $85\times$ reduction in data.

经过成功的、目标导向的演示训练的动作条件视频生成模型往往会忽略命令的动作，无论对它们提出什么要求，都会呈现出看似合理的专家式未来。我们将这种失败追溯到缺乏足够的行动影响力的训练数据。我们分析这个指标并证明它限制了任何基于该数据训练的模型可以表达的动作影响力。专家演示的行动分布很窄，可以在不关注行动的情况下预测下一个状态，这设定了一个很低的上限，仅靠模型容量是无法提高的。提高它需要收集不同的数据。我们引入了行动影响力最大化（AIM），这是一种主动收集训练数据以提高数据集行动影响力的算法。我们将 AIM 的性能和数据效率与模拟导航和操作任务中的探索启发法和内在动机方法进行比较。在基于像素的操纵中，AIM 是唯一能够持续提高可控性的方法：将 500 个主动收集的片段添加到 3k 演示中，与基于 300k 专家片段训练的关于末端执行器可控性的世界模型相匹配，数据减少了 85 倍。

## 19. Ropedia W0: Scaling Loco-Manipulation World Models across Embodiments with Human Experience / Ropedia W0：通过人类经验扩展局部操纵世界模型

**Categories**: cs.CV, cs.RO

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

We present Ropedia W0, the first egocentric loco-manipulation world foundation model spanning humans and robots with diverse embodiments. We curate Xp-Data, comprising 10k hours of egocentric human data and 3k hours of robot data with unified multimodal annotations and systematic quality control. A unified kinematic representation encodes locomotion through camera poses and manipulation through projected skeletons, with robot skeletons combining embodiment-specific forward kinematics with extensive manual keypoint annotation and correction. We also introduce Xp-Teleop, a long-horizon loco-manipulation benchmark based on high-quality dexterous humanoid teleoperation data. Applications in policy evaluation, model-based planning, and world-action modeling further demonstrate the model's utility for embodied decision-making.

## 20. Simulation Priors for Rapid Adaptation in Model-Based RL for Autonomous Racing / 自主赛车基于模型的强化学习中快速适应的仿真先验

**Date**: 2026-10-19 | **arXiv**: [2610.26757v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26757v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.26757v1)

**Categories**: cs.LG, cs.RO, eess.SY

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Reinforcement learning enables learning high-performance control policies, but its reliance on extensive interaction limits deployment on physical systems where data collection is costly, and failures are unsafe. We introduce Prior-Informed Adaptation (PIA), an offline-to-online model-based RL framework that uses structured simulation priors for rapid adaptation. PIA pretrains a world model on heterogeneous low- and high-fidelity simulated trajectories, then adapts it with limited target-domain interaction using uncertainty-regularized planning. Rather than requiring an accurate simulator for policy transfer, PIA treats simulated trajectories as biased priors whose residual mismatch is corrected during online adaptation. We evaluate PIA in autonomous racing using high-fidelity simulation and on a real full-size autonomous race car. In simulation, PIA adapts faster across track and surface shifts than strong baselines, achieving lower lap times and fewer failures. On hardware, PIA achieves reliable few-shot adaptation with less than one hour of on-vehicle interaction.

强化学习能够学习高性能控制策略，但它对广泛交互的依赖限制了在数据收集成本高昂且故障不安全的物理系统上的部署。我们引入了先验适应（PIA），这是一种基于离线到在线模型的强化学习框架，它使用结构化模拟先验来进行快速适应。 PIA 在异构低保真度和高保真度模拟轨迹上预训练世界模型，然后使用不确定性正则化规划使其适应有限的目标域交互。 PIA 不需要精确的模拟器来进行策略转移，而是将模拟轨迹视为有偏差的先验，其残余不匹配在在线适应期间得到纠正。我们使用高保真模拟和真实的全尺寸自动驾驶赛车来评估自动驾驶赛车中的 PIA。在模拟中，PIA 比强基线更快地适应赛道和路面变化，从而实现更短的单圈时间和更少的故障。在硬件上，PIA 通过不到一小时的车载交互实现了可靠的小样本自适应。
