## 1. Learning to Correct Geometry in Generated Videos / 学习校正生成视频中的几何图形

**Date**: 2026-10-16 | **arXiv**: [2610.23856v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23856v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.23856v1)

**Categories**: cs.CV

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Generated videos often lose geometric consistency under camera motion: structures warp under viewpoint change, parallax becomes inconsistent, and smooth camera movement may collapse into shot-like transitions. We argue that this failure is largely a data problem. In web-scale video corpora, most visible motion comes from dynamic objects captured by static or weakly moving cameras, leaving only sparse supervision for viewpoint-induced appearance changes. To address this, we propose Video Geometry Corrector (VGC), a post-hoc video-to-video model trained on synthetic paired supervision. Starting from real videos with diverse camera motion and pose annotations, we construct VG-Pairs with a synthetic paired-supervision data generation pipeline. Each video clip is treated as a geometry-consistent target and paired with a distorted counterpart synthesized by a pretrained image-to-video inpainting model using trajectory-adaptive keyframes. VGC learns to map the distorted input to the geometry-consistent target without modifying the source generator, and uses optical flow as an auxiliary cue to help disentangle camera motion from object motion. Experiments on pose-conditioned and prompt-driven benchmarks show that VGC improves geometric consistency across multiple source generators, and scaling to a larger backbone further extends the framework toward more general video refinement.

生成的视频在摄像机运动下通常会失去几何一致性：结构在视点变化下翘曲，视差变得不一致，并且平滑的摄像机运动可能会崩溃成镜头般的过渡。 我们认为，这种失败在很大程度上是一个数据问题。 在网络规模的视频语料库中，大多数可见运动来自静态或弱移动摄像机捕获的动态物体，只留下对视点引起的外观变化的稀疏监督。 为了解决这个问题，我们提出了视频几何校正器（ VGC ） ，这是一种经过合成配对监督训练的事后视频到视频模型。 从具有不同摄像机运动和姿态注释的真实视频开始，我们使用合成配对监督数据生成管道构建VG-Pairs。 每个视频剪辑都被视为几何一致的目标，并与由使用轨迹自适应关键帧的预训练图像到视频修复模型合成的失真对应物配对。 VGC学习在不修改源生成器的情况下将失真输入映射到几何一致的目标，并使用光流作为辅助线索，以帮助将摄像机运动与物体运动分开。 对姿势调节和提示驱动基准的实验表明， VGC提高了多个源生成器的几何一致性，并且扩展到更大的主干进一步扩展了框架，以实现更通用的视频细化。

## 2. Persistent Planar Memory for Video World Models / 视频世界模型的持久平面内存

**Date**: 2026-10-16 | **arXiv**: [2610.23795v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23795v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.23795v1)

**Categories**: cs.CV

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Video world models that generate and explore environments at scale need a persistent 3D memory of what they have produced. Plane primitives offer a natural candidate: a compact set of oriented planes captures the dominant structural elements (e.g., walls, floors, facades) of indoor and outdoor scenes, while requiring only 7 parameters per primitive. We present PPMem, a video world model that adopts plane primitives as its persistent 3D memory. PPMem augments a video diffusion transformer with a planar prediction head and a Depth Token Fusion (DTF) module: each autoregressive chunk jointly produces RGB video and planar geometry, which merges into a persistent plane memory that conditions subsequent chunks via rendered depth. The resulting memory is three orders of magnitude more compact than per-pixel point clouds (~100K primitives, 5MB for a full scene), geometrically explicit (metric depth, normals, exportable surfaces), cross-view consistent (jointly predicted by the shared backbone), and adaptive (refined by the merging procedure as generation proceeds). On DL3DV and RealEstate10K, PPMem outperforms prior 3D-grounded world models in video quality while jointly producing metric-scale depth, surface normals, and exportable planar geometry, sustaining coherence across hundreds of frames under large viewpoint changes. Project page: https://hyzcluster.github.io/ppmem.

大规模生成和探索环境的视频世界模型需要对其生成的内容具有持久的3D内存。 平面原语提供了一个自然的候选：一组紧凑的定向平面捕捉了室内和室外场景的主要结构元素（例如，墙壁、地板、立面） ，而每个原语只需要7个参数。 我们展示了PPMem ，这是一个采用平面原语作为其持久3D内存的视频世界模型。 PPMem增加了具有平面预测头和深度令牌融合（ DTF ）模块的视频扩散变压器：每个自回归块共同产生RGB视频和平面几何体，其合并到永久平面存储器中，该存储器通过渲染深度调节后续块。 由此产生的内存比每像素点云（约100K原语，完整场景为5MB ）、几何显式（度量深度、法线、可导出表面）、交叉视图一致（由共享主干共同预测）和自适应（随着生成过程的进行，通过合并过程进行细化）紧凑三个数量级。 在DL3DV和RealEstate10K上， PPMem在视频质量方面优于先前的3D接地世界模型，同时联合生成公制尺度深度、表面法线和可导出的平面几何形状，在大视点变化下保持数百帧的一致性。 项目页面： https://hyzcluster.github.io/ppmem。

## 4. Agentic Physics-Grounded Video Generation via Memory-Augmented In-Context Reasoning / 通过内存增强的上下文推理进行基于代理物理学的视频生成

**Date**: 2026-10-16 | **arXiv**: [2610.23677v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23677v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.23677v1)

**Categories**: cs.CV

**Project**: https://xxkkxxx.github.io/PhysRepair-Website/.  <details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Physical plausibility is essential for realistic video generation. Existing methods impose physical guidance before synthesis via simulations or refined prompts, but this can limit dynamic diversity, miss errors that emerge only after generation, and require repeated regeneration when inconsistencies remain. We therefore study a generate-diagnose-repair paradigm that preserves the original generation capability and selectively repairs physical inconsistencies afterward, raising two key questions: what to repair and how to repair? To address them, we introduce PhysRepair, an agentic video generation framework that combines causal reasoning over the observed physical failure with prior repair experience to enable localized and adaptive correction. Specifically, Spatiotemporal Causal Constraints identify the first invalid physical state and its causally affected states, producing a repair specification that guides physically plausible local regeneration while preserving unaffected content. Experience-augmented In-context Learning combines the ongoing repair context with relevant experience from previous repairs, allowing the agent to adapt its repair decisions across diverse physical failures and accumulate completed trajectories for future cases. Experiments on Physics-IQ Verified across solid mechanics, fluid dynamics, optics, thermodynamics, and magnetism demonstrate that PhysRepair consistently improves the physical plausibility of generated videos over existing methods. Our project page is available at https://xxkkxxx.github.io/PhysRepair-Website/.

物理合理性对于逼真的视频生成至关重要。 现有的方法通过模拟或精确的提示在合成之前施加物理指导，但这可能会限制动态多样性，错过仅在发生后才出现的错误，并且当不一致性仍然存在时需要重复再生。 因此，我们研究了一种生成-诊断-修复范式，该范式保留了原始发电能力，并在之后有选择地修复物理不一致性，提出了两个关键问题：修复什么以及如何修复？ 为了解决这些问题，我们引入了PhysRepair ，这是一个代理视频生成框架，将观察到的物理故障的因果推理与之前的修复经验相结合，以实现局部和自适应校正。 具体而言，时空因果约束识别第一个无效的物理状态及其因果影响状态，产生一个维修规范，指导物理上合理的局部再生，同时保留未受影响的内容。 经验增强的上下文学习将正在进行的维修环境与以前维修的相关经验相结合，使代理能够在各种物理故障中调整其维修决策，并为未来的案例积累完整的轨迹。 经过固体力学、流体动力学、光学、热力学和磁学验证的Physics-IQ实验表明， PhysRepair与现有方法相比，始终如一地提高了生成视频的物理合理性。

## 6. I2V-DETACH: Source Grounding Detachment for Unauthorized Image-to-Video Generation / I2V-DETACH ：用于未经授权的图像到视频生成的源接地分离

**Date**: 2026-10-16 | **arXiv**: [2610.23287v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23287v1) | **PDF**: [Link](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.23287v1)

**Categories**: cs.CV

<details><summary><b>Abstract / 摘要</b></summary>

Recent diffusion models have advanced image-conditioned video generation, but their accessibility raises risks of unauthorized manipulation. Image immunization has emerged as a promising defense for Image-to-Video (I2V) protection, yet existing methods often rely on degrading visual quality or temporal coherence. In this work, we revisit I2V immunization as reducing source-content preservation, shifting the focus from output degradation to weakening source-conditioned content propagation. To this end, we propose I2V-DETACH, an image immunization method that prevents unauthorized I2V generation through source grounding detachment. I2V models preserve source content by propagating source-image cues into video latents during denoising. Accordingly, I2V-DETACH disrupts this process in two complementary ways: it suppresses source-video grounding to reduce source cue propagation, and strengthens intra-video interactions to shift denoising toward video-side dynamics with reduced reliance on the source image. To obtain informative optimization signals, we initialize auxiliary video latents with source-oriented states that remain within the source-conditioned generation regime while deviating from source-preserving trajectories. Extensive experiments across diverse diffusion architectures show that I2V-DETACH substantially reduces source-content preservation, improves over prior immunization methods, and exhibits strong black-box transferability.

最近的扩散模型具有先进的图像条件视频生成，但其可访问性增加了未经授权操作的风险。 图像免疫已经成为图像到视频（ I2V ）保护的一种有希望的防御手段，但现有方法往往依赖于降低视觉质量或时间一致性。 在这项工作中，我们重新审视了I2V免疫作为减少源内容保存，将焦点从输出降解转移到削弱源条件内容传播。 为此，我们提出了I2V-DETACH ，这是一种通过源接地分离防止未经授权产生I2V的图像免疫方法。 I2V模型通过在去噪过程中将源图像线索传播到视频潜伏期来保留源内容。 因此， I2V-DETACH以两种互补的方式破坏了这一过程：它抑制源视频接地以减少源提示传播，并加强视频内交互以减少对源图像的依赖，从而将去噪转向视频端动态。 为了获得信息优化信号，我们初始化辅助视频潜伏，其源导向状态保持在源条件生成模式中，同时偏离源保留轨迹。 对不同扩散架构的广泛实验表明， I2V-DETACH大大减少了源物质的保存，改进了先前的免疫方法，并表现出强大的黑盒可转移性。
